THE LTT STANDARD

Four Pillars of Uncompromised Testing

Most travel guides are compiled by content aggregators or influencers flown out on VIP press trips. We operate like undercover consumer inspectors.

Zero Freebies

100% Self-Funded Stays

We pay standard consumer rack rates with our own money, booking anonymously under everyday personal aliases. No comped suites, no free airport champagne, and zero PR agency scripts. What we experience is precisely what paying guests receive.

Boots On Ground

Field-Tested Reality

We examine what glossy promotional videos edit out: room acoustics, mattress ergonomics, peak breakfast buffet crowd flow, check-in responsiveness, and pool lounger availability under full resort occupancy.

Data-Backed

200,000+ Reviews Audited

A single visit is just one data point. We systematically cross-examine our on-site findings against 200,000+ verified customer reviews across Booking.com and Google to isolate genuine chronic issues from rare one-off anomalies.

Reader First

Clear Financial Transparency

Complete honesty: when you book through our verified partner links, we may receive a modest commission at no extra cost to you. This funds our anonymous stays. We never accept payment to inflate a hotel's ranking or soften a candid review.
