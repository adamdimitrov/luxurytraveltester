10 Best Theme Parks in Dubai in 2026 (and the Abu Dhabi Parks Worth the Trip)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 theme park tickets compared on price, indoor vs outdoor, thrill level and transfers
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most visitors the best theme park in Dubai is IMG Worlds of Adventure at €63: 22 rides fully indoors and air-conditioned, so the heat never decides your day, and children under 105 cm get in free. Want two parks in one day? The Dubai Parks Multi Parks Pass (€88) adds a second park for about €15.

On IMG Worlds vs Motiongate: IMG wins on price and comfort, Motiongate (€73) on coasters, with 5 of them among 27 rides at Dubai Parks and Resorts, 25 minutes from Dubai Marina. For young children, LEGOLAND Dubai is built around LEGO play, from Driving School to the LEGO Factory.

The names many people search for, Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World and SeaWorld, are not in Dubai. They are on Yas Island in Abu Dhabi (Warner Bros. World and SeaWorld quote about 40 minutes from Dubai), and their tickets include no transport from Dubai. Below are 5 Dubai options and 5 Abu Dhabi ones, including the only one with hotel pickup from Dubai.

Practical Tips: Heat, Multi-Park Passes and Getting There

Heat: when to pick an indoor park

Three parks here are sold as indoor: IMG Worlds in Dubai (indoor and temperature-controlled), and Warner Bros. World and SeaWorld on Yas Island. LEGOLAND Dubai mixes indoor and outdoor attractions. The Motiongate, Real Madrid World and Ferrari World tickets do not say, so on a hot day plan around the indoor parks, or use Real Madrid World's late hours: it is open from 12:00 to 21:00.

Combining parks with multi-park passes

At Dubai Parks and Resorts, the Multi Parks Pass gives two parks on the same day for €88, and the gates are a 5–10 minute walk apart. On Yas Island, the Multi-Park Pass gives 2, 3 or 4 parks over 6 days, which is the better fit for a stay in Abu Dhabi.

What costs extra

No ticket on this page includes food, and IMG Worlds and Ferrari World do not allow outside food or drinks. Quick Pass upgrades are sold separately at the Yas Island parks. Watch for park-specific extras: the Kids Zone at IMG Worlds, karting and F1 simulators at Ferrari World and the animal encounters at SeaWorld.

Getting there

Dubai Parks and Resorts (Motiongate, LEGOLAND, Real Madrid World) is on Sheikh Zayed Road, about 25 minutes from Dubai Marina; Motiongate's ticket includes free parking, and none of these tickets includes transport. IMG Worlds has free parking but no pickup. The Yas Island parks are in Abu Dhabi (Warner Bros. World and SeaWorld quote 40 minutes from Dubai); the single-park tickets include no transport and the free shuttle is suspended until further notice, so you need a car, a taxi or the day trip with hotel pickup.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best theme park in Dubai?

IMG Worlds of Adventure for most visitors: it is fully indoor and air-conditioned, has 22 rides across Marvel, Cartoon Network and dinosaur zones, costs from €63 and lets children under 105 cm in free. For coasters pick Motiongate; for young children pick LEGOLAND Dubai.

IMG Worlds vs Motiongate: which is better?

IMG Worlds is cheaper (€63 vs €73), fully indoors and better in the hot months. Motiongate has more rides (27, including 5 roller coasters) and 15 live shows, and you can add a second Dubai Parks park for about €15 with the Multi Parks Pass.

How much are theme park tickets in Dubai?

From €63 for IMG Worlds and €73 each for Motiongate, LEGOLAND Dubai and Real Madrid World. Two Dubai Parks parks on one day cost €88. In Abu Dhabi, Ferrari World and Warner Bros. World start at €86, SeaWorld at €73 and the Yas 2-park pass at €108.

Are Ferrari World and Warner Bros. World in Dubai?

No. Both are on Yas Island in Abu Dhabi, with SeaWorld; Warner Bros. World and SeaWorld quote about 40 minutes from Dubai. Their single tickets include no transport. From Dubai, the day trip with hotel pickup (€127) includes the return drive, the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque and entry to Ferrari World, Warner Bros. World or SeaWorld.

Which Dubai theme parks are indoor?

IMG Worlds of Adventure is the fully indoor, temperature-controlled park in Dubai. LEGOLAND Dubai mixes indoor and outdoor attractions. In Abu Dhabi, Warner Bros. World and SeaWorld are indoor parks.

Is a multi-park pass worth it?

Yes if you will really visit two parks. At Dubai Parks and Resorts, two parks on the same day cost €88 instead of €73 for one. On Yas Island, the 2-park pass from €108 works out at about €54 per park and is valid for 6 days, so it suits a stay in Abu Dhabi more than a day trip. For water parks on the same passes, see our Dubai water parks comparison.

Can I cancel Dubai theme park tickets?

All ten tickets on this page offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before, and all five Dubai tickets and the Abu Dhabi day trip also let you reserve now and pay later.
