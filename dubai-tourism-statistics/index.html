Dubai Tourism Statistics 2026 (How Many Tourists Visit?)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
Compiled from official Dubai DET data • 5 Charts with Data Tables • Methodology
Last updated: Nov 19, 2026

Dubai welcomed 19.59 million international overnight visitors in 2025, a third record year in a row and 17% more than before the pandemic. Its 154,264 hotel rooms were 80.7% full on average, at an average rate of AED 579 (about $158) a night.

This report covers who visits Dubai, where they come from, when they come, how full the hotels are and what a room costs, all from the Department of Economy and Tourism (DET). For the whole country, see our UAE tourism statistics, and for the capital, Abu Dhabi tourism statistics. Every chart has a data table and a link to its official source.

How many tourists visit Dubai each year?

Dubai welcomed 19.59 million international overnight visitors in 2025, about 870,000 more than in 2024. It was the third record year in a row: 2023 (17.15 million) was the first year above the 2019 peak of 16.73 million.

Growth is slowing as the base gets bigger: +19.4% in 2023, +9% in 2024 and +5% in 2025. In absolute terms Dubai still adds close to a million visitors a year, roughly the population of a mid-sized European city arriving on top of last year's total.

DET counts international overnight visitors: people from abroad who stay at least one night in a hotel or hotel apartment. Day trippers, transit passengers who never leave DXB and UAE residents visiting from other emirates are not included, which is why Dubai's figure is not directly comparable with Abu Dhabi's broader "visitors" total.

Where do Dubai's tourists come from?

Western Europe sent 4.10 million visitors in 2025 (21% of the total), up from 3.74 million in 2024. The Gulf states (GCC, 2.99 million), South Asia and CIS and Eastern Europe (2.89 million each) each supply about 15%.

No single region dominates: the top four together account for two thirds of all visitors. Neighbouring Gulf and wider Middle East markets (GCC plus MENA) add up to 26%, more than Western Europe on its own.

By country, DET's headline releases do not publish a full-year 2025 ranking. India and the United Kingdom are consistently named as the two largest single markets; the UK alone sent about 1.04 million visitors in the first nine months of 2025, 120,000 more than a year earlier (Gulf News, citing DET).

When is Dubai busiest?

Dubai's high season runs from November to March, when daytime temperatures are in the mid-20s °C. December 2025 set the all-time monthly record with 2.04 million visitors, beating January 2025 (1.94 million). Summer is far quieter: June 2025 had about 1.2 million visitors.

December drew 70% more visitors than June. That gap is the best argument for a shoulder-season trip: in late October, early November or April the weather is still good for the beach and the desert, and hotels are easier to book than over Christmas and New Year.

Outdoor experiences follow the same calendar. Desert evenings, hot air balloon flights and open-deck dhow cruises are at their best (and busiest) between November and March, so that is when our desert safari comparison, hot air balloon guide and Dubai dinner cruise ranking are worth reading early.

How full are Dubai's hotels?

Dubai's hotels were 80.7% occupied on average in 2025, up from 78.2% in 2024 and 77.4% in 2023, even though the number of rooms kept growing. Hotels sold 44.85 million room nights in 2025.

Room supply barely moved in 2025 (+248 rooms) while demand rose 4%, which is why occupancy jumped 2.5 points. Dubai now has more hotel rooms than New York, Paris or Singapore and almost as many as London, and an occupancy above 80% across that stock is unusually high for a city of its size.

Visitors stayed 3.7 nights on average in 2025, the same as in 2024. That is short enough that most trips are city-break length, which is why the first-timer staples (the Burj Khalifa observation decks, a city tour and one desert evening) sell out first.

How much does a hotel room in Dubai cost?

The average daily rate (ADR) was AED 579 in 2025 (about $158 at the fixed rate of 3.6725 dirhams per dollar), up 8% from AED 538 in 2024. Revenue per available room (RevPAR) rose 11% to AED 467, the fastest growth of any headline hotel figure.

Rates were almost flat in 2024 (+AED 2) and then jumped AED 41 in 2025, when hotels could raise prices because they were fuller. The averages hide a wide range: they include budget hotels and hotel apartments as well as the Palm Jumeirah resorts, where peak-season nights cost several times the city average (see our Palm Jumeirah hotel comparison).

How does Dubai compare with Abu Dhabi and the UAE?

Dubai holds about seven in ten UAE hotel rooms (154,264 of roughly 217,000) and dominates international arrivals. Abu Dhabi, the capital, had 5.9 million hotel guests in 2025 and 81% occupancy, with stronger growth in rates (ADR +19%) than Dubai. The full comparison is on our UAE tourism statistics page; the capital's own figures are on Abu Dhabi tourism statistics.

Dubai International Airport (DXB) handled 92.3 million passengers in 2024 and has been the world's busiest airport for international passengers for 11 years in a row, according to Airports Council International. Many of those passengers are in transit, which is one reason DET counts overnight hotel guests rather than arrivals.

Frequently asked questions

How many tourists visited Dubai in 2025? 19.59 million international overnight visitors, according to Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism, up 5% on 2024.

Which country sends the most tourists to Dubai? By region, Western Europe (4.10 million in 2025). By single country, India and the United Kingdom are the largest markets; DET has not published a full-year 2025 country ranking.

What is the busiest month in Dubai? December. December 2025 was the first month with more than 2 million visitors (2.04 million).

How long do tourists stay in Dubai? 3.7 nights on average in hotels in 2025.

How many hotel rooms does Dubai have? 154,264 rooms in 827 hotels and hotel apartment buildings at the end of 2025.

  • Earlier years: DET 2023 release (WAM, February 2024) for 2023 and 2019, and the 2022 release for 2022 and 2021.
  • Monthly figures: DET releases for January to May 2025 and the first half of 2025; June and the second half are derived by subtraction.
