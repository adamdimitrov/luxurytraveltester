THE LTT STANDARD

Why Our Reviews Actually Matter

Most travel guides are written by copywriters aggregating press releases or influencers flown out on paid junkets. We do things differently.

100% Self-Funded Stays

We book anonymously under personal names and pay standard consumer rack rates out of our own pocket. No comped suites, no free champagne, and zero PR strings attached.

Hands-On Property Audits

We evaluate what brochures gloss over: room comfort, peak breakfast crowd management, service responsiveness, pool chair availability, and whether high-ticket amenities actually justify their nightly rate.

200K+ Reviews Cross-Audited

Our on-site stay is just one data point. We cross-examine our findings against 200,000+ verified customer reviews across Booking.com and Google to isolate genuine trends from rare one-off anomalies.
