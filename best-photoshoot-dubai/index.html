9 Best Photoshoots in Dubai in 2026 (Private Photographers and Flying Dress Shoots)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
9 Dubai photoshoots compared on price, edited photos, delivery time, flying dress and pickup
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most visitors the best photoshoot in Dubai is the private shoot with hotel pickup at €143 per person: the photographer drives you to the spots you pick and delivers around 50 edited photos per location. Couples on a tighter budget get a private Burj Khalifa shoot with 25 edited photos in 48 hours for €94 for two.

Here for the flying dress? The cheapest shoot with the dress included is the Burj Khalifa flying dress shoot at €211 for two: one dress delivered to your hotel, 20–25 edited photos and two edited reels within 24 hours. For dunes instead of towers, the private desert flying dress trip (€499 for two) adds pickup and a dune drive.

The camera is not what separates these shoots. How many photos are edited, how fast they arrive, whether the dress is included and who gets you to the spot are: edited counts below run from 14 to about 150, and delivery from 24 hours to 25 days. All 9 are compared side by side below.

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

If you have seen the flying dress shots on Instagram, this is the cheapest way on this page to get one with the dress included: €211 for up to 2 people. You choose the dress from a catalogue, it arrives at your hotel the day before, and you meet the team at the Wings of Mexico statue in Downtown.

The hour is a tight walking loop past the spots that frame the Burj Khalifa best: Dubai Mall Park Bridge, Burj Park, the Dandelion sculpture, Burj Ceni and Dubai Opera, about 10 minutes each. You leave with 20–25 edited photos, a 20–25 second reel and a short story clip, all within 24 hours, plus every raw file. That is the fastest turnaround here.

The catch is the dress itself: it only flies if someone lifts and throws it. Couples do that for each other; solo travellers pay 200 AED for an assistant, which brings the price close to €260. If you want to go up the tower before or after, see our Burj Khalifa ticket comparison.

  • Included: 1-hour private videography and photo walk, 1 flying dress, 20–25 edited photos, 1 edited 20–25 s reel, 1 edited 7–10 s story clip, all raw photos and videos

Practical Tips: Flying Dresses, Timing and What Costs Extra

How the flying dress works

The "flying" effect is a person lifting and throwing the train while the photographer shoots, so bring a partner or pay for an assistant (200 AED on the #2 pick). Dresses are chosen from a catalogue after booking; on the #2 pick it is delivered to your hotel the day before. A second dress costs 350 AED there, while Floganza includes two. Only #2, #3 and #8 include a dress in the price; #1, #5 and the add-on menus elsewhere charge for one.

Where to shoot

Most city shoots start at the Wings of Mexico statue or Souk Al Bahar in Downtown and walk a loop with the Burj Khalifa behind you: Dubai Mall Park Bridge, Burj Park, the Dandelion sculpture, Burj Ceni and Dubai Opera. If you also want to go up the tower, book that separately (compare Burj Khalifa tickets): no shoot here includes admission. For dunes, the desert shoots handle transport; for Old Dubai's souks and abras, Picster is the only listing here that names it. For a broader sightseeing day, see our Dubai city tours.

Heat and what to bring

Most shoots here are outdoors. Listings ask you to bring comfortable shoes, sunglasses, a hat, water and sun protection, and the #1 pick suggests a change of clothes. In the hot months, ask your photographer for an early or late slot when you book. Sunrise over the desert from the air is another option: see hot air balloon flights.

What costs extra

Check these before you pay: hotel transfers (included only on #1, #3 and #8), flying dress hire, a solo assistant, extra photos or premium edits, drone footage, short videos and make-up or hair. None of the shoots includes food or drinks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a photoshoot in Dubai cost?

Private shoots start at about €80 for a group (Picster, smallest package) and €94 for a couple (Burj Khalifa couple shoot with 25 edited photos). A private shoot with hotel pickup is €143 per person. Flying dress shoots run from €211 for two in Downtown to €499 for two in the desert and €799 per person for Floganza.

Where can I do a flying dress photoshoot in Dubai?

The two classic settings are Downtown, with the Burj Khalifa behind you (from €211 for two, dress included, meeting at the Wings of Mexico statue), and the desert dunes (from €499 for two with pickup and a dune drive). Floganza also shoots in the desert, with two dresses, for €799 per person.

Is the flying dress included?

Only on three of the nine shoots: the Burj Khalifa flying dress shoot (1 dress), the desert flying dress and dune drive trip (1 dress) and Floganza (2 dresses). Elsewhere it is an add-on, for example 200 AED per person on the #1 pick.

How long does it take to get the photos?

From 24 hours to about 25 days. The Burj Khalifa flying dress shoot delivers edited reels within 24 hours; the couple shoot, Picster and the Marina tour deliver within 48 hours; Pictrip within 5 working days. The desert shoots are slowest: about two weeks for #3 and up to 25 days for Floganza's retouched photos.

What is the best couple photoshoot in Dubai?

For value, the Burj Khalifa couple and pre-wedding shoot: €94 for two, 25 edited photos within 48 hours, or 30 plus raw files on the 2-hour option. For a flying dress, the €211 Burj Khalifa shoot is priced for two and needs a partner to throw the dress anyway.

Can I hire a photographer for a proposal in Dubai?

Yes. The proposal photoshoot in Downtown (€130 for up to 10) has the photographer hide and step out at the moment, with 50 edited photos. The Pictrip personal photographer (€263 for up to 6) plans the locations to your brief.

Do Dubai photoshoots include hotel pickup?

Only three do: the private photoshoot with hotel pickup (#1), the desert flying dress and dune drive (#3) and Floganza (#8). The Downtown shoots meet at Wings of Mexico or Souk Al Bahar, and the Marina tour at Stefano's in Dubai Marina.
