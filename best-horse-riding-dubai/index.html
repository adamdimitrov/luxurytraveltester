7 Best Horse Riding Experiences in Dubai in 2026 (Desert, Beach and Oasis Rides Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
7 horse rides compared on price, ride length, setting, rider level, weight limits and pickup
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For a first desert ride, book the 1-hour JSR Equestrian Club ride at €45: a full hour on open sand, helmet and chaps provided, horses matched to complete beginners and confident riders alike. If you have no car, the Al Marmoom Oasis ride from €34 is the one with a hotel pickup option, and the most-booked ride on this page.

Three things decide which ride is right. Weight: four of the five desert stable rides cap riders at 85 kg; Al Marmoom allows 91 kg. What the price really covers: Al Marmoom's €34 buys 30 minutes, and a guide to assist your ride costs 100 AED more on site. Setting: every Dubai ride is in the desert. The beach rides people search for are in Umm Al Quwain and Ras Al Khaimah, cost €397 and up, and are private.

Below: 5 desert stable rides from €34 to €57 and 2 private beach rides. Filter by setting, first-timer friendly, private or pickup.

This is the most-reviewed ride on the page, and the price explains why: €34 gets you into a working Arabian stud in the Al Marmoom desert oasis, a farm tour, an Arabian horse exhibition and a 30-minute ride through the oasis, with coffee and dates afterwards. Where it wins is families and mixed groups: children aged 9 and under visit free, pony rides can be added, and the horse show keeps non-riders entertained. The weight limit is 91 kg, the most generous of the stable rides. Dress code is strict: long trousers, closed-toe shoes with a small heel, nothing loose or dangling.

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

Do not be misled by the name: the meeting point for this beach ride is in Al Jazeera Al Hamra, Ras Al Khaimah, not Dubai. Once there you get a 2-hour private ride along open shoreline on a calm Arabian or mixed-breed horse, with professional handlers and guides speaking Arabic and English.

It is pitched at intermediate riders and confident beginners, the only listing here that is not sold to complete first-timers. That is the point: the open stretches are there for cantering. If you ride at home and want more than a walk, this is the one.

At €542 per person (down from €570) it is the priciest ride on the page, about €271 an hour. The listing does not mention helmets or transport. If you just want a beach ride and do not need two hours, the Umm Al Quwain ride is about €144 cheaper.

Which Horse Ride Should You Book?

  • First ride, best value: JSR desert hour. A full 60 minutes for €45 with helmet and chaps.
  • No car, or riding with young children: Al Marmoom Oasis, the only stable ride with a pickup option; under-10s visit free and pony rides can be added.
  • Nervous beginner who wants a lesson feel: M11, with certified instructors and an arena before the desert.
  • Golden-hour dune photos: Dune Hill. Every slot is sunrise or sunset, maximum 5 riders, photos on your phone.
  • Something to post, or a longer trail: M11 with the Ride-Thru Açaí, which also offers a 2-hour hack.
  • Beach ride, any level: Umm Al Quwain, private, 3 hours on a quiet coast.
  • Beach ride for experienced riders: Sunset Beach in Ras Al Khaimah, 2 private hours with room to canter.

Over 85 kg? Only Al Marmoom (91 kg) lists a higher limit; the beach listings state none, so ask the operator before booking.

Practical Tips: Timing, What to Wear and Getting There

Best time to ride

The stables sell sunrise and early-evening slots: JSR offers morning (pitched as the cooler slot) or evening, M11 sunrise or early evening, and Dune Hill only sunrise or sunset. In the hot months, book the earliest slot you can face. M11's indoor arena means its rides go ahead year-round.

What to wear and bring

Most stables ask for long trousers and closed-toe shoes; M11 and Al Marmoom refuse sandals and open-toed shoes, and Al Marmoom asks you to avoid skirts, scarves and dangling jewellery. Helmets are provided at the desert stables; at Umm Al Quwain they come on request. For the Umm Al Quwain beach ride, pack a change of clothes and clothes that can get dirty.

Weight and age limits

The most common limit is 85 kg (JSR, M11, Dune Hill). Al Marmoom allows up to 91 kg. Most stables take children from age 5, and Al Marmoom lets under-10s visit free. Expect to sign a waiver at M11.

Getting there

JSR, M11 and Dune Hill are desert stables outside the city, in the Al Ruwaiyah area for JSR and M11, and none includes pickup, so budget for a taxi or drive. Al Marmoom offers a transfer option. The two beach rides are in Umm Al Quwain and Ras Al Khaimah, north of Dubai. For a stay out in the desert, see our best desert resorts in Dubai.

What costs extra

Al Marmoom's guide is 100 AED per person, paid on site, and pony rides for children are extra. M11 does not include riding boots or chaps. Gloves are sold separately at Umm Al Quwain.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the best place to go horse riding in Dubai?

For most visitors, the desert stables in the Al Ruwaiyah area. JSR Equestrian Club offers a full 1-hour desert ride from €45 with helmet and chaps. If you need hotel pickup, Al Marmoom Oasis (from €34) is the stable ride that offers it.

How much does horse riding in Dubai cost?

Desert rides cost €34 to €57 per person: €34 for 30 minutes at Al Marmoom (plus 100 AED for a guide on site), €45 for an hour at JSR or 45 minutes at Dune Hill, and €55 to €57 at M11. Private beach rides cost €397 to €542 per person.

Can you ride horses on the beach in Dubai?

None of the beach rides on this list is in Dubai itself. The two bookable options are a private 3-hour ride in Umm Al Quwain (from €397), where the itinerary also lists swimming, and a private 2-hour ride in Al Jazeera Al Hamra, Ras Al Khaimah (from €542) for intermediate riders.

Can complete beginners go horse riding in the Dubai desert?

Yes. Every desert ride on this page is sold for beginners and experienced riders, with a safety briefing and a horse matched to your level. M11 adds certified instructors and an arena option. Only the Ras Al Khaimah beach ride asks for intermediate riders or confident beginners.

Is there a weight limit for horse riding in Dubai?

Yes. JSR, M11 and Dune Hill cap riders at 85 kg, and Al Marmoom at 91 kg. The beach ride listings state no limit, so check with the operator before booking.

Can children go horse riding in Dubai?

Most stables take children from age 5, including JSR and M11. At Al Marmoom, children aged 9 and under visit the stud free and pony rides can be added for an extra cost.

What should I wear for horse riding in Dubai?

Long trousers and closed-toe shoes; sandals and open-toed shoes are refused at M11 and Al Marmoom. Helmets are provided at the desert stables. For a beach ride bring a change of clothes, sunscreen, a hat and water.
