10 Best Luxury Hotels in Dubai in 2026 (Prices Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Dubai 5-star hotels compared on nightly price, guest score, beach, breakfast and room size
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most couples the luxury hotel to book in Dubai is Banyan Tree at Bluewaters, €570 a night with breakfast: a 9.5 guest score and 500 m of private beach. The icon everyone searches for, Atlantis The Royal, is €746 with breakfast; the best city hotel is Waldorf Astoria DIFC at €430; and Raffles at €233 gives you a 70 m² room with a terrace for the lowest price here.

The catch most visitors miss: the nightly rate is not what you pay. A 10% service charge, 7% municipality fee, 5% VAT and an AED 20 tourism fee come on top, and only six of the ten include breakfast. All 10 were compared on price for the same night, guest score, beach, breakfast and the size of the cheapest room. Booking a Luxury Hotel in Dubai: Fees, Extras and Location Trade-offs

What the headline price leaves out

  • Service charge: the room rates add a 10% service charge, so Atlantis The Royal's €746 becomes €821 and Le Royal Meridien's €312 becomes €344.
  • Taxes: on top of that come 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and the Dubai tourism fee of AED 20 per room per night (AED 40 on some Banyan Tree family rooms), paid at the hotel.
  • Breakfast: included at Banyan Tree, Atlantis The Royal, Al Qasr, One&Only, Jumeirah Beach Hotel and Le Royal Meridien; €40 at Address Downtown; on rates from €483 at Waldorf Astoria.
  • Extra beds: free at Four Seasons (3–17), Al Qasr (to 11) and Banyan Tree (7+); AED 200–400 a night elsewhere; not available at Atlantis The Royal or One&Only.

Beach clubs and waterparks

  • Included in the rate: daily Wild Wadi Waterpark at Al Qasr and Jumeirah Beach Hotel (plus kids' club at both), waterpark access on One&Only's rooms.
  • On site: Atlantis The Royal has beach clubs and sits next to Aquaventure World; Four Seasons has cabanas and day villas for in-house guests.
  • No beach: Waldorf Astoria, Raffles and Address Downtown. Plan a taxi to the coast or a day at a resort.

Beach or city?

  • Palm and Bluewaters (Atlantis The Royal, One&Only, Banyan Tree): the most resort-like, but far from the old town; Atlantis The Royal is 22 miles from Dubai International.
  • Jumeirah coast (Four Seasons, Al Qasr, Jumeirah Beach Hotel): beach plus easier city access; Four Seasons is 15 minutes from Burj Khalifa.
  • City (Waldorf Astoria, Address Downtown, Raffles): walkable sights and short airport runs (Waldorf Astoria is 11 km and Raffles 15 minutes' drive from Dubai International), but beach days need a taxi. Waldorf Astoria and Raffles have metro access.

Age rules

Atlantis The Royal and One&Only The Palm need the person checking in to be 21; most others ask for 18; Four Seasons and Jumeirah Beach Hotel have no minimum age.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best luxury hotel in Dubai?

For most couples, Banyan Tree Dubai at Bluewaters: €570 a night with breakfast, a 9.5 guest score and 500 m of private beach. For a city base, Waldorf Astoria DIFC scores the same 9.5 for €430. The most talked-about resort is Atlantis The Royal at €746 with breakfast.

What is the most luxurious hotel in Dubai you can book?

Of the ten compared, One&Only The Palm (€1,030 with breakfast, two-Michelin-star STAY by Yannick Alléno) and Jumeirah Al Qasr (€1,041 with breakfast, 2 km of private beach) are the most expensive. Atlantis The Royal is the most extravagant, with 16 restaurants and a rooftop sky pool.

How much does a 5-star hotel in Dubai cost per night?

For two adults on a January weeknight, the ten hotels here ran from €233 (Raffles) to €1,041 (Jumeirah Al Qasr), with a median of about €590. Add a 10% service charge, 7% municipality fee, 5% VAT and an AED 20 tourism fee per night.

Which luxury hotels in Dubai include breakfast?

Six of the ten include breakfast in their cheapest rate: Banyan Tree (€570), Atlantis The Royal (€746), Jumeirah Al Qasr (€1,041), One&Only The Palm (€1,030), Jumeirah Beach Hotel (€521) and Le Royal Meridien (€312). At Address Downtown breakfast is €40; at Waldorf Astoria DIFC breakfast rates start at €483.

Which luxury hotel in Dubai is best for families?

Four Seasons Jumeirah Beach for space (70 m² rooms, free extra beds for 3–17), Jumeirah Beach Hotel for value (€521 including breakfast, kids' club and daily Wild Wadi), and Jumeirah Al Qasr for the palace setting with free extra beds to 11. More options in our Dubai family resorts guide.

Should I stay on the beach or in Downtown Dubai?

Stay on the beach (Banyan Tree, Four Seasons, Le Royal Meridien) for a resort holiday, and in the city (Waldorf Astoria DIFC, Address Downtown, Raffles) for sightseeing, shopping and short stays: Waldorf Astoria is one metro stop from Burj Khalifa and Raffles 15 minutes' drive from Dubai International. Four Seasons is the compromise, 15 minutes from Burj Khalifa.

Do Dubai luxury hotels have age limits?

Yes, some. Atlantis The Royal and One&Only The Palm require the guest checking in to be at least 21 and offer no extra beds. Most others require 18, while Four Seasons Jumeirah Beach and Jumeirah Beach Hotel have no minimum check-in age.
