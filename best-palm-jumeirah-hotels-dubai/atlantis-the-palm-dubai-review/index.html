Pros:

  • Best All-Around — This 5-star hotel has everything for a complete Palm hotel experience.
  • Best Value for Families — This hotel outperforms all other hotels in terms of what you pay vs. what you get if you have kiddos.
  • Excellent Rooms — The standard Palm facing rooms are spacious, extravagant, well-equipped, and luxurious. Let alone the higher-tier rooms.
  • Waterpark Entry Included — Enjoy free access to Aquaventure World, featuring exciting water slides, tunnels, and falls, along with a private beach.
  • Royal Treatment — From the moment you set foot in the Palm Atlantis, every hotel staff member strives to make your stay as pleasant as possible. You'll truly feel like a cherished guest. They go above and beyond.
  • Free Buffet — Enjoy a diverse selection of expertly prepared cuisines for breakfast, lunch, or dinner, including Indian, Arabic, Mexican, British, Japanese, Lebanese, seafood, and steaks.
  • Michelin Star Restaurants — You can access Michelin-star restaurants on-site, such as Nobu, Hakkasan, FZN, or Studio Frantzén (for an additional charge).
  • Family Friendly — An insane variety of entertainment options are available for kids and teenagers, including the Lost Chambers Aquarium, Aquaventure World, the Dubai Balloon, arcade rooms, bowling, game rooms, and the Explorer's Club, science-focused youth programs, and more.
  • Parent Friendly — Parents can enjoy some well-earned child-free relaxation, knowing their children are safe with first-aid and CPR-trained guardians and having the time of their lives in Atlantis' babysitting, childcare facilities, and youth programs.
  • Instagram Worthy — If you want to show off on Instagram, this place has plenty of great high-status shots.

Cons:

  • Doors Aren't Soundproof — Noise from the hallway, including slamming doors, conversations, and children's cries, can be heard inside the room.
  • Unstable Wi-Fi — My calls on instant messaging apps were frequently cut short due to connection issues. Laptop work was also frustrating, as pages loaded slowly or not at all.

I booked the King Palm Room for a 5-day stay for 9,659.79 AED (€2432.15).

I found the Palm Atlantis experience to be the best hotel experience for most people, especially first-time visitors to Dubai. Everybody should experience it at least once in their lives.

It is affordable, offers a wide range of activities and dining options, and provides the best value for your money.

Atlantis The Palm is a luxury 5-star resort located at the center of Palm Jumeirah's crescent, with direct access to Aquaventure Waterpark and the Palm Monorail.

I chose to stay in the most affordable option, the Palm King Room, which offers views of the city and the central part of Palm Jumeirah, rather than the Oceanside view with little to see.

The room was elegant and budget-friendly (€400-500, all-inclusive), featuring a comfortable bed, a fabulous bathroom, a practical work desk, and stunning views of Palm Jumeirah and the Dubai cityscape.

The service was consistently exceptional. The staff went above and beyond to assist me at every juncture, and they were hospitable and welcoming, including the housekeeping, security, and restaurant personnel.

Housekeeping even surprised me with small gifts, such as a mousepad and a sunglass cloth.

Dining options are extensive, with 15 restaurants onsite, including buffets and restaurants offering Indian, Arabic, Japanese, Italian, British, Mexican, Lebanese, seafood, and more.

Facilities span lush outdoor gardens, private beaches, pools, lounges, a full aquarium, a spa, a gym, and kids' clubs.

The main drawbacks were poor WiFi and noise from the hallway due to inadequate door insulation, which seems to be a common issue in hotels.

The overall experience is designed for fun, comfort and enjoyment, with a continuous focus on guest satisfaction. There’s always something to do here.

The Palm Atlantis provides excellent value on Palm Island, especially for families. It's the best-all-around option for everyone, including couples, groups, and or solo travellers (like me).

If I'm in Dubai again, I'd absolutely return.

🛎️ Check-In & Check-Out Logistics ▾
  • Check-in starts at 3 PM, and check-out is at 11 AM. The process was quick and efficient.
  • As soon as I exited my Uber, the staff took my luggage to my room and provided me with a ticket to track it.
  • Early check-in is available but depends on room availability, especially for Atlantis Unlocked members. If you inform the hotel during booking, you might get lucky. I didn't request early check-in, yet I was accommodated by 11 AM.
  • Check-out is at 12 PM. If you are a member of Atlantis Unlocked (membership is free), you can request a late checkout. You can leave your luggage in your room, and the staff will bring it down to your car at the entrance, ensuring a true 5-star experience.
🛏️ Palm King Room & Balcony Views Accommodations ▾
  • I stayed in a Palm King Guest Room, which offers a direct view to the inner courtyard, Palm Island and the Dubai skyline.
  • Ocean View rooms face the Persian Gulf, but the view is not as captivating as the Palm view, which offers a stunning panorama of Palm Island and the Dubai skyline.
👑 Queen vs. King Room Room Value ▾
  • King rooms feature a single large bed, making them ideal for couples or solo travelers seeking comfort and space.
  • Queen rooms are equipped with two queen beds, making them ideal for families. They can comfortably accommodate either two adults and two children or three adults and one child.

🏛️ Indoor Areas Architecture ▾

Lobby

  • The Grand Lobby at Atlantis The Palm is the first thing you see—and it’s absolutely out of this world. Towering at 19 meters, it features a jaw-dropping Dale Chihuly glass sculpture, marble floors, cascading water walls, and ocean-inspired design.
  • It’s the kind of place that looks incredible on Instagram and makes your friends instantly wish they were there.

Lounges

  • The hallways at Atlantis The Palm carry the ocean theme with grand columns, domed arches, and sea-inspired decor, making the whole place feel luxurious. Right next to the Grand Lobby, you can relax in a stylish café with amazing pastries or chill in a lounge open to everyone.

Shops

  • Atlantis The Palm’s The Avenues shopping area features 35+ luxury stores, offering everything from high-end fashion (Tiffany & Co., Montblanc, Via Rodeo) to beachwear (Vilebrequin, Hamac) and souvenirs (Legends of Atlantis, Dolphin Bay Boutique).
  • You'll also find specialty shops like Rivoli for watches, Yateem Optician for eyewear, and Marina Pharmacy for essentials—all without leaving the resort.

The Lost Chamber's Aquarium

  • The Lost Chambers Aquarium at Atlantis The Palm is an immersive, Atlantis-themed underwater world with over 65,000 marine animals across 21 exhibits.
  • Walk through glass tunnels to see sharks, rays, and seahorses, or take part in interactive touch tanks, behind-the-scenes tours, and even snorkeling or diving in the Ambassador Lagoon.
  • Open every day 10 AM–9 PM, it’s free for resort guests and kids under 3.
🏖️ Outdoor Areas Pools & Beach ▾

Aquaventure World Waterpark

  • Aquaventure World, included free with your stay at Atlantis The Palm, is the world’s largest waterpark and a must-visit. With over 105 slides and attractions, it features record-breaking thrills like the Leap of Faith and Odyssey of Terror, plus family rides, rapids, splash zones, and a 1km private beach.
  • I tried it all—it’s a blast, though be ready for lots of stair-climbing and queues. Kids will love the massive Splashers play areas, while marine lovers can enjoy encounters with dolphins, sea lions, rays, and sharks at Atlas Village.

Garden

  • Step outside at Atlantis The Palm and you’ll find palm-lined paths, lush gardens, and beautiful views of the Arabian Gulf. It’s a peaceful, photo-worthy setting—perfect for a quiet walk, relaxing moment, or just enjoying the resort’s calm, tropical vibe.

Pools

  • The Grand Pool offers a more laid-back, spacious setting surrounded by palm trees and luxury loungers. It’s ideal for adults or anyone looking to relax in a quieter atmosphere, although it's not adults-only (i.e. there are children around).
  • The Family Pool at Atlantis The Palm is perfect for kids and parents alike. With shallow areas, lifeguards, and a fun, energetic vibe, it’s a safe and exciting place for children to splash around while adults relax nearby.

Free Private Beach

  • As a guest at Atlantis The Palm, you get free access to the resort’s private beach. Enjoy complimentary sun loungers, umbrellas, towels, and beachside food and drink service. Swimming is safe and family-friendly, and water sports like kayaking and paddleboarding are available for an extra fee.

White Beach (Extra Cost)

  • WHITE Beach is Atlantis The Palm’s upscale beach club offering a chic adults-only vibe with stunning views, stylish loungers, and two infinity pools. It’s not included for hotel guests.
  • Part of the fee is usually redeemable on food and drinks.
  • By day, it’s perfect for sunbathing and Mediterranean dining. By night, it transforms into a vibrant party spot with live DJs and a trendy crowd.

🎡 Other Facilities Amenities ▾

Gym (AWAKEN Fitness Centre)

  • The AWAKEN Fitness Centre at Atlantis The Palm is equipped with state-of-the-art Technogym equipment for cardio, strength, and flexibility training. Guests can benefit from personal trainers, virtual classes, and a dedicated fitness studio. The center is open daily from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM.

AWAKEN Spa

  • The AWAKEN Spa at Atlantis The Palm offers a unique holistic experience blending aromatherapy, UAE-made skincare, and healing sound frequencies to cleanse the body and uplift the mind and spirit.‍
  • Signature treatments use high-frequency wellness essences, and guests can unwind in the sauna, steam room, cold plunge, vitality pool, and relaxation lounge.

Explorers' Club

  • The Atlantis Explorers Club at Atlantis The Palm is a top-tier kids' facility for ages 4–12, featuring ten themed zones like cooking workshops, creative crafts, interactive games, and role-play villages. It blends fun with education through expert-led programs on marine life and conservation.
  • For toddlers (0–3), there's a safe, supervised play area. Staffed by multilingual childcare pros trained in pediatric first aid, it ensures a secure and enriching experience. Read more about it here.
⭐ Service & Hospitality Service Verdict ▾
  • The staff at Palm Atlantis are exceptionally friendly and polite.
  • The cafeteria and restaurant staff were always prompt in seating me upon arrival. They quickly brought refreshments and coffee and were attentive to any requests I had. I appreciated their occasional check-ins to ensure everything was satisfactory.
  • As you exit the elevator to the ground floor, a security guard is always there to greet you with a smile and a friendly 'good day.' In the main hall, staff are more than willing to assist with taking photos as well. Additionally, there's a public area downstairs after the underground aquarium, where a two-man security checkpoint ensures that only hotel guests can enter.
  • I consistently experienced smooth and friendly service from the receptionists and concierge during both check-in and check-out.
  • I appreciated the thoughtful gestures from the room cleaning staff, who gifted me a free mouse pad and a glass cleaning cloth, and often tidied up and organized my room. All of this was unprompted, but it made my stay more special.
