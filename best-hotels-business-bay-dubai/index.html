10 Best Hotels in Business Bay, Dubai in 2026 (Prices Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Business Bay hotels and apartments compared on price, guest score, room size, pools and breakfast
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most stays in Business Bay, book Embassy Suites by Hilton at €186 a night: it is the only 5-star here with breakfast for two in its cheapest rate, plus an infinity pool and free cancellation. On a budget, Park Regis gives you a canal-view room and a rooftop pool for €91; for a splurge, SLS Dubai at €212 has 46 m² balcony rooms and seven restaurants.

Business Bay gets you a 9.0-rated 5-star from €109 (Hotel Indigo) and a 9.2 with breakfast for €186 (Embassy Suites). Downtown puts you next to Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall. ME by Meliá is 2.4 km from Dubai Mall and Park Regis 3 km from Burj Khalifa. Compare both in our Downtown Dubai hotels guide.

What is the best serviced apartment in Business Bay?

For score and service, InterContinental Residences (9.5, 48 m² studio with kitchen, €210). For value, Lusso Hometels Missoni (9.3, 41 m² studio with balcony and washing machine, €129).

Which Business Bay hotel is best for families?

DAMAC Maison Aykon City has three pools, a kids' club and kitchenette studios for €130. Embassy Suites adds a children's buffet and play areas with breakfast in the rate, and Canal Central has a kids' pool and free access to a private beach on the Palm.

Are there beach hotels in Business Bay?

No, Business Bay is on the canal. Two hotels bridge the gap: Canal Central's Superior Room includes free access to its private beach on Palm Jumeirah, and Barceló runs a free shuttle to Kite Beach. For hotels right on the sand, see the best hotels in JBR.

What taxes are added to Dubai hotel prices?

At the 5-star hotels here, a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and an AED 20 per night tourism fee are added to the room price. Apartments and some hotels also take a refundable damage deposit of AED 200–750.
