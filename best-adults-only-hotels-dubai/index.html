10 Best Adults-Only & All-Inclusive Hotels in Dubai in 2026 (1 Adults-Only, 9 All-Inclusive, Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Dubai hotels compared on all-inclusive price, room-only price, child policy, beach, room size and guest score
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For an all-inclusive in Dubai, book Amwaj Rotana on JBR at €270 a night for two: beach 100 steps away, a 42 m² sea-view room and all meals for just €84 more than room-only. The cheapest all-inclusive is Towers Rotana at €205 (city, metro opposite), the cheapest on a beach is Riu at €207, and for a spa splurge it's The Retreat Palm at €376.

The catch: only one hotel here actually bans children, voco Dubai Monaco at €134 (half board €271), and it has no all-inclusive rate. Many hotels tagged adults-only still welcome kids under their house rules. All 10 were compared on the all-inclusive and room-only price for the same night, child policy, beach and pools, room size and guest score. 📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

Amwaj Rotana is the all-inclusive to look at first in Dubai. For €270 a night for two you get a newly renovated 42 m² sea-view room with a balcony, the beach about 100 steps away and The Walk at JBR outside the door. The same room costs €186 room-only, so all your meals for two add €84 a night, the smallest jump on this page after Towers Rotana.

It also has the best position of the beach options: a 9.5 location score, a tram stop three minutes away, Jumeirah Lake Towers metro nearby and a free mall shuttle. That matters on an all-inclusive, because the hotel's three restaurants (Horizon, Benihana and Rosso) are only part of what is on your doorstep.

Compared with Sheraton The Walk (#4, €273) nearby, Amwaj is €3 cheaper, sits closer to the sand and scores higher on location; the Sheraton has slightly bigger rooms (45 m²) and lets pets stay. Compared with Riu (#3, €207), you pay €63 more for a 5★ hotel, a bigger room and a far better location.

The catch: this is a family-friendly hotel, with a children's pool and slide and the Splashpad water park five minutes away. If kids at breakfast would spoil it, look at voco Dubai Monaco (#2). Taxes and charges are added to the €270.

  • Beach: Direct access to Jumeirah Beach, about 100 steps

Adults-Only in Dubai: What the House Rules Actually Say

Dubai has very few hotels that keep children out. Many properties carry an adults only tag in search filters, yet their own house rules say “children of any age are welcome”; the tag often means only that the minimum check-in age is 18. We checked the child policy of every hotel in this comparison, and of the long list behind it.

  • Truly adults-only: #2 voco Dubai Monaco. Children are not allowed, no cots or extra beds, minimum check-in age 18.

Booking All-Inclusive in Dubai: Where, Getting Around and Extra Costs

Where to stay

  • JBR (Amwaj Rotana, Sheraton The Walk): the best location scores (9.2–9.5), beach, tram and The Walk's restaurants on foot. Best if you will still eat out some nights.
  • Mina Seyahi and Al Sufouh (W, Westin): big resorts with their own restaurants and beach, where all-inclusive makes most sense.
  • Palm Jumeirah (The Retreat): quieter, 10 minutes by car to the Marina.
  • Deira Islands (Riu) and The Heart of Europe (voco): self-contained beach stays away from the centre.
  • Sheikh Zayed Road and Dubai Creek (Towers Rotana, Al Bandar Arjaan): city hotels, no beach, close to the metro or airport.
  • The desert (Al Marmoom): 36 km from Al Maktoum International.

Getting around

Amwaj Rotana is three minutes' walk from the JBR 2 tram stop, and Towers Rotana is directly opposite Financial Centre Metro. Towers Rotana runs a free shuttle to Kite Beach and Amwaj a free mall shuttle. Elsewhere, plan on taxis: the Westin puts JBR Walk at 5 minutes and Burj Khalifa at 20 minutes by car. Note voco Dubai Monaco's entry is closed 22:00–07:30.

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: every price above excludes taxes. Where the rate shows the breakdown, a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and a Dubai tourism fee of AED 15–20 per room per night are added.
  • Deposits: AED 500 at voco Dubai Monaco (card) and Towers Rotana (cash).
  • Extra beds: AED 120–216 a night where offered; voco has none and Riu has none.
  • Nights out: all-inclusive covers the resort only. If you plan a dinner cruise or a desert evening, a breakfast or half-board rate is usually the better buy for those dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there adults-only all-inclusive hotels in Dubai?

Not among the hotels we compared. The only one whose house rules ban children is voco Dubai Monaco, The Heart of Europe, and it offers breakfast and dinner (€271 for two) rather than all-inclusive. For all-inclusive with a calmer, adult feel, look at The Retreat Palm (€376) or the W Mina Seyahi (€471), both of which still accept children.

What is the cheapest all-inclusive hotel in Dubai?

Towers Rotana on Sheikh Zayed Road, at €205 a night for two on a January weeknight, opposite Financial Centre Metro. The cheapest on a beach is Riu Dubai Beach Resort on Deira Islands at €207. Taxes and the Dubai tourism fee come on top.

How much more does all-inclusive cost than room-only in Dubai?

For two people per night: €42 at Towers Rotana, €84 at Amwaj Rotana, €147 at Sheraton The Walk, €174 at The Retreat Palm and €223 at the Westin Mina Seyahi. At the top end, half board is often the better deal.

Which all-inclusive resorts in Dubai are on the beach?

Amwaj Rotana (beach about 100 steps away), Riu Dubai (private beach area), The Retreat Palm (on the beach, Palm Jumeirah) and the Westin Mina Seyahi (500 m private beach). The W Mina Seyahi has an infinity pool, with Mina Seyahi Beach 400 m away.

Are children allowed at voco Dubai Monaco?

No. Its house rules say children are not allowed, the minimum check-in age is 18 and there are no cots or extra beds. Entry to the property is also closed between 22:00 and 07:30.

Is the Dubai tourism fee included in all-inclusive rates?

No. The all-inclusive prices on this page exclude taxes. Where the rate shows the breakdown, a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and a tourism fee of AED 15–20 per room per night are added at booking or at the hotel.
