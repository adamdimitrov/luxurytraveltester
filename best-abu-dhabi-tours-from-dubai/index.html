10 Best Abu Dhabi Day Trips from Dubai in 2026 (Grand Mosque, Louvre and Palace Tours Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Abu Dhabi day trips compared on price, length, entries included, lunch, group size and dress code
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most people the best Abu Dhabi day trip from Dubai is the €45 Premium Full-Day Sightseeing Tour: hotel pickup, a guided visit inside the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, the Corniche, Emirates Palace and a Louvre photo stop, with a guide in one of five languages. Only want the mosque? The 5-hour half-day tour costs the same and gives you the afternoon back.

What decides the booking is what you actually go inside. Most tours from €30 show you the Louvre, Qasr Al Watan and Ferrari World from the kerb. If the Louvre Abu Dhabi matters, book one of the three tours with the museum ticket included (€57–85). For the presidential palace interior, pick a tour with Qasr Al Watan entry.

Below, 10 tours compared on price, length, entries included, lunch, group size and the mosque dress code, with a side-by-side table and filters for Louvre, palace and budget trips.

Practical Tips: Dress Code, Fridays and What Costs Extra

The mosque dress code

Every tour enforces it, and failing it means no entry. Men: long trousers and tops that cover the shoulders; no shorts or sleeveless shirts. Women: long, loose clothing covering arms and legs, nothing see-through, and the hair covered. Tattoos must be covered. Most tours lend women an abaya and headscarf; none include clothing for men (#6 can arrange it). If you forget, traditional clothing can be bought at the mosque.

Fridays, holidays and flight days

The Emirates Palace coffee tour's listing notes the mosque closes to visitors on Fridays from 11:30 to 14:30, so its itinerary is rearranged; check the order of stops on any Friday tour. Entry is not guaranteed on Islamic holidays, and Qasr Al Watan can close without notice for presidential events (most tours then swap in the Louvre). Several listings warn against booking on the day you fly: the drive is about 1–1.5 hours each way and traffic can delay drop-off.

What costs extra

Lunch is not in any from-price here: it is either an option or a break at your own cost. The Louvre is a photo stop unless the tour includes entry (#3, #4, #8), and Qasr Al Watan entry comes only as an option, on #1 (Premium) and #6. Tips are never included, and luggage is either not allowed or charged.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Abu Dhabi day trip from Dubai?

For most people the Premium Full-Day Sightseeing Tour at €45: hotel pickup, a guided visit inside the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, the Corniche, Emirates Palace and a Louvre photo stop, with guides in five languages. Its Premium option adds Qasr Al Watan and the Etihad Towers deck.

Which Abu Dhabi tours from Dubai include Louvre entry?

Three: the Grand Mosque & Louvre Full-Day (€69, 2 hours inside), the small-group Grand Mosque & Louvre tour (€57, about 2 hours, max 10 people) and the Mosque, Zayed Museum & Louvre tour (€85). On the others the Louvre is a photo stop or not visited.

How long is an Abu Dhabi day trip from Dubai?

The drive takes about 1–1.5 hours each way. A mosque-only half day takes about 5 hours door to door; full-day tours run 7–10 hours including transfers.

What should I wear to the Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque?

Men: long trousers and covered shoulders, no shorts or sleeveless tops. Women: loose clothing covering arms and legs, nothing see-through, and a headscarf. Tattoos must be covered. Most tours lend women an abaya; no tour includes clothing for men in the price.

Is lunch included on Abu Dhabi tours from Dubai?

Not in the from-price of any tour here. Some offer lunch as an option (an international buffet, Al Khayma Heritage Restaurant or a checkout add-on); on the others there is a lunch break at your own cost.

Can I visit the Grand Mosque on a Friday?

Yes, but one listing notes it closes to visitors from 11:30 to 14:30 on Fridays, so tours change the order of stops. Muslim travellers can join the Friday Prayer tour, which is timed for Jumu'ah at 12:45.

Can I combine Abu Dhabi sightseeing with Ferrari World?

Several tours here include a Ferrari World photo stop only; #6 and #7 build it into the base itinerary. For a day trip with a park ticket and the mosque, see our theme parks guide.
