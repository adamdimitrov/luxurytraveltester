8 Best Burj Khalifa Tickets in 2026 (124/125 vs 148 vs 152 vs Combos)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
8 Burj Khalifa tickets compared on floors, fast-track, time on deck, extras and price per extra floor
Last updated: Nov 16, 2026

Book SKY Level 148 at €99 if this is your first time up the Burj Khalifa. It costs €52 more than the standard 124/125 ticket, but that buys fast-track through security and lifts, a deck capped at 50 people, lounge snacks, and 124/125 is still included. On a budget, the standard Levels 124/125 ticket at €47 shows you the same skyline.

The ticket to skip is fast-track 124/125 at €78. For just €21 more, 148 skips the same queues and adds 23 floors and the lounge. At the other end, the Level 152 Lounge at €190 is the highest terrace you can book (575 m), but you pay for the seat and the glass of bubbly, not a different view.

Below: all 8 tickets side by side, including the Aquarium, Burj Club dinner and Sky Views combos, a table of what each upgrade costs per extra floor, and when sunset slots are worth the premium.

🏆 #1 Best Overall
At the Top SKY • ⏱️ 1.5 hours • 📍 Dubai Mall, Downtown Dubai

Burj Khalifa SKY Level 148, 124 & 125 Entry Ticket

4.6(24.7k+)
From €99
▾

Pros:

  • Fast-track through security and lifts, the biggest time saver in the building
  • Includes Levels 124 and 125, so you lose nothing versus the standard ticket
  • Deck capped at 50 people, far calmer than 124/125
  • Lounge snacks and hot drinks included
  • Sunset option available for golden hour

Cons:

  • More than double the standard 124/125 price
  • View is from higher up but similar in character to 124/125
  • Visit capped at 1.5 hours
  • No food or drinks before Iftar during Ramadan
📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the ticket that solves the two complaints most people have about the Burj: queues and crowds. You skip the line at security and at the lift, start in the SKY Lounge with snacks and hot drinks, and step out on a Level 148 terrace where no more than 50 people are allowed at once. Then you still get Levels 124 and 125 as part of the same visit.

Against the standard ticket at €47, you pay about €52 more, roughly €2.25 per extra floor. Against the fast-track 124/125 ticket at €78, the gap is only €21, and for that you add 23 floors, the lounge and the guest ambassador. That makes 148 the better buy than fast-track 124/125 for almost everyone.

Be clear about what the extra money buys. Mostly it is fewer people and no queuing: the view from 148 is higher, but similar in character to 124/125. If calm space at the window and no queuing are worth €52 to you, book it. If you want the absolute top of the building, the Level 152 lounge goes higher, at almost twice the price.

Pick the sunset option if golden hour matters to you, and time your slot about 30 minutes before a fountain show (evening shows run every 30 minutes from 6 pm to 11 pm) to see it from above.

  • Included: SKY Level 148 and Levels 124/125, skip-the-line security and lift, introductory tour with a guest ambassador, lounge with snacks and hot drinks, interactive stations, Wi-Fi

  • Accessibility: Wheelchair-accessible

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the baseline everyone compares against: Levels 124 and 125 for €47, the world's fastest lift, the open-air terrace on 124 at 452 metres and telescopes on 125. For most visitors the view from here is the view they came for.

What you give up is comfort. There is no fast-track, so you go through the regular security and lift lines, and there can be queues at the deck exhibits too. Sunset is the busiest time on these shared decks, so getting a clear spot at the window takes patience at golden hour.

The options matter: you can add a café treat, a souvenir, or the Dubai Aquarium at booking. If you think you might want the aquarium, compare this with the dedicated Aquarium combo below at €79.

Book it if price is the main factor or you are visiting outside sunset. If you would pay €31 to skip the queues, skip straight to the 148 ticket instead: it costs only €21 more than fast-track 124/125 and includes a lot more.

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

The Lounge is a different kind of visit. Instead of standing at a crowded window, you sit in a lounge across levels 152, 153 and 154, with canapés, unlimited teas, coffee and soft drinks, and one glass of bubbly or a house drink. A host takes you onto the outdoor terrace at 575 metres, about 120 metres higher than the 124 terrace.

The price is the catch: €190, or €91 more than the 148 ticket. That is roughly €23 for each of the four extra floors over 148, against about €2.25 per floor to go from 125 to 148. You are paying mainly for the setting, the seat and the drink, not for a radically different view.

It is worth it when the occasion matters: an anniversary, a proposal, a first evening in Dubai where you want to sit through sunset rather than stand. A late-afternoon slot lets you see the city in daylight and after dark within the 2-hour visit.

Levels 124/125 are not part of this ticket's inclusions, so treat this as a lounge experience, not an all-decks pass. If you want every floor plus fast-track at a lower price, the 148 ticket is the smarter buy.

Which Burj Khalifa Ticket Should You Book?

  • First visit, want it done right: SKY Level 148 at €99. Fast-track, calm 50-person deck, lounge snacks, and 124/125 included.
  • On a budget, visiting in the morning or afternoon: standard Levels 124/125 at €47. Same skyline, more people.
  • Anniversary, proposal or a treat: Level 152 Lounge at €190. Highest terrace (575 m), a seat and a drink.
  • Family with kids: Aquarium + 124/125 at €79. Two attractions in the same mall, aquarium at any time that day.
  • Want dinner too: Burj Club meal at €82. A 3-course meal facing the fountain for €35 over the standard ticket.
  • Want the Burj in your photos: 148 + Sky Views at €121, or 124/125 + Sky Views at €69 if a non-prime slot is fine.

The one ticket we would steer most people away from is fast-track 124/125: it only makes sense if €78 is your hard limit.

Sunset Slots, Fountain Shows and Timing

Every ticket is tied to a time slot, and the slot is the decision that matters most after the tier. What to know:

  • Sunset is the prime window. The 148 ticket offers a dedicated sunset option, and the cheapest Sky Views combo is restricted to non-prime hours. Sunset is the slot most people want, so book it as early as you can and check the price for that time.
  • Sunset on 124/125 is crowded. It is the busiest time on the shared decks, and the window spots go first. If sunset is the point of your visit, 148 (max 50 people) or the 152 Lounge (seated) is where the extra money pays off most.
  • Fountain shows: daytime shows at 1 pm and 1:30 pm (Fridays 1:30 pm and 2 pm), evening shows every 30 minutes from 6 pm to 11 pm. Book your slot about 30 minutes before the show you want to see, to get a good spot.
  • Visit length: about 1.5 hours on 124/125 and 148, 2 hours in the Lounge, 1 hour on the fast-track ticket.

Getting There, What to Bring and What Costs Extra

Getting there: the At the Top entrance is on the lower ground floor of Dubai Mall (Unit LG-089). Dubai Mall is huge, so allow extra time to find the entrance before your slot.

What to bring: a passport or ID card (the standard and Lounge tickets ask for it, the Sky Views combo asks for photo ID).

What costs extra: the café treat and souvenir on the standard ticket (optional at booking), drinks at the Burj Club, and the Sky Glass Slide at Sky Views (25 AED per person). During Ramadan, the 148 lounge serves no food or drinks until Iftar.

Kids: under-3s are free on the standard, Aquarium and 148 + Sky Views tickets, under-4s on the 124/125 + Sky Views combo. In the 152 Lounge, children 3 to 16 must be with an adult.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a Burj Khalifa ticket?

The standard Levels 124 and 125 ticket starts from about €47 per adult. Fast-track 124/125 is about €78, SKY Level 148 (with 124/125) about €99, and the Level 152 Lounge about €190. Combos range from €69 (with Sky Views) to €121 (148 with Sky Views).

Is Level 148 worth it over 124/125?

For most first-time visitors, yes. For about €52 more you get fast-track security and lifts, a deck limited to 50 people, lounge snacks and hot drinks, and 124/125 is still included. The view itself is similar in character, so if you only want the skyline and do not mind queues, 124/125 is enough.

What is the difference between 148 and 152?

Level 148 is an observation deck with an outdoor terrace, fast-track and a snack lounge for about €99. The Lounge on levels 152 to 154 is a seated lounge with a glass of bubbly or a house drink, canapés, unlimited soft drinks and a 575 m terrace, for about €190. You pay roughly €91 more for four extra floors and a lounge setting.

What is the best Burj Khalifa and Aquarium combo?

The Aquarium + Levels 124/125 combination ticket at about €79 includes the aquarium tunnel, Underwater Zoo and Underwater Observatory. Both must be used on the same day; the Burj is at your booked slot and the aquarium any time that day.

Do I need fast-track at the Burj Khalifa?

At sunset and in busy periods, it helps. But fast-track 124/125 only (€78) is just €21 cheaper than SKY Level 148 (€99), which also includes fast-track. If you are going to pay for skipping the queue, 148 is the better buy.

When is the best time to visit the Burj Khalifa?

Sunset is the most popular slot and the most crowded on 124/125. Book it early, or pick 148 or the 152 Lounge if sunset is your priority. For the fountain, book about 30 minutes before an evening show; they run every 30 minutes from 6 pm to 11 pm.

Can I cancel a Burj Khalifa ticket?

All eight tickets compared here offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before for a full refund. The 148, 148 + Sky Views and 152 Lounge tickets also let you reserve now and pay later.
