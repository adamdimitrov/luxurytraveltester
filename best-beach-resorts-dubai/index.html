10 Best Beach Resorts in Dubai in 2026 (Prices Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Dubai beach resorts compared on nightly price, guest score, beach, pools, kids' club and breakfast
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most couples the Dubai beach resort to book is Banyan Tree on Bluewaters at €570 a night, with breakfast, a 500 m private beach and the joint-top guest score of the ten (9.5). The best value on the sand is Sheraton JBR at €231 with breakfast, families should look at Le Meridien Mina Seyahi (€275, kids' club and free waterpark), and the splurge is Four Seasons Jumeirah at €868 for 70 m² rooms.

The catch most visitors miss: plenty of "beach hotels" in Dubai only run a shuttle to the sand. Nine of the 10 here sit on their own beach, from Jumeirah and JBR to Bluewaters and the Creek; the tenth, Mileo on the Palm, gives free access to a private beach 200 m away. They were compared on price for the same night, guest score, beach type, pools, kids' club, breakfast and room size. Staying on the Beach in Dubai: Areas, Getting Around and Extra Costs

Which beach to stay on

  • Jumeirah and Umm Suqeim (Four Seasons, Nikki Beach, Mina Al Salam): closest to Downtown and Dubai International; Four Seasons is 15 minutes' drive from Burj Khalifa, Nikki Beach 15 minutes from the airport. Mina Al Salam shares Madinat Jumeirah's 2 km beach.
  • Al Sufouh and Mina Seyahi (Le Meridien): the long family beach with Jungle Bay waterpark and Barasti; Mall of the Emirates is 8 km.
  • Bluewaters (Banyan Tree): an island beach under Ain Dubai, across the bridge from JBR.
  • JBR (Sheraton, Address, Al Habtoor Grand): beach plus The Walk's restaurants and shops outside the door.
  • Palm Jumeirah (Mileo): the budget way onto the Palm, with free private beach access 200 m from the hotel.
  • Dubai Creek Harbour (Vida Creek Beach): a creek beach 10 km from Dubai International, best for short stays.

Getting around

The JBR, Bluewaters and Palm resorts list Al Maktoum International as the nearest airport (21–30 km), but most flights land at Dubai International: Vida Creek Beach is 10 km away, Nikki Beach about 15 minutes, Four Seasons about 20 km and Le Meridien 31 km. Parking is free at all ten, and Mina Al Salam runs shuttles to Mall of the Emirates and Souk Madinat Jumeirah.

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: the nightly prices shown exclude them. Where the rates break them down, a 10% service charge (10.5% at Le Meridien), 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and a Dubai tourism fee of AED 20 per room per night are added on top.
  • Breakfast: included at six of the ten; €33 at Le Meridien (room-only rates) and Al Habtoor Grand; not shown for Four Seasons, Mileo and Vida Creek Beach.
  • Extra beds: free at Four Seasons (ages 3–17), Mina Al Salam (to 11), Banyan Tree (7+, who pay adult rates) and Nikki Beach (4–5); AED 125–400 a night elsewhere, with Address the highest.
  • Deposits: the Sheraton collects AED 1,000 in cash on arrival, refunded at check-out.

On the water

The rates here are for mid-January, the cooler winter season. Most of these beaches are a short ride from the marinas: compare jet ski tours, scuba diving in Dubai and dinner cruises if you want to see the coast from the sea.

What is the best beach resort in Dubai?

Banyan Tree Dubai at Bluewaters, from €570 a night with breakfast. It shares the highest guest score of the ten compared (9.5, with Mileo), a 500 m private beach and a 54 m² entry room. For more space and three pools, Four Seasons Resort at Jumeirah Beach (€868) is the step up.

How much does a beach resort in Dubai cost per night?

On a January weeknight for two adults, the ten resorts here ran from €148 (Vida Creek Beach, a 4★ on the creek) to €967 (Jumeirah Mina Al Salam, with breakfast). The median was about €355. A 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and an AED 20 tourism fee per night come on top.

Le Meridien Mina Seyahi at €275 with breakfast: it has a kids' club with two kids' pools, a 1,200 m beach and free unlimited Jungle Bay waterpark access. Can children use the infinity pool at Address Beach Resort?
