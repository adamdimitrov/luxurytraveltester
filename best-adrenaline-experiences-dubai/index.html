6 Best Adrenaline Experiences in Dubai in 2026 (Skydiving, Parasailing, Jet Cars Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
6 Dubai thrills compared on price, adrenaline level, time in the air or on the water, and age and weight limits
Last updated: Nov 16, 2026

If you can spend €679 and weigh under the limit, book the tandem skydive over the Palm. Nothing else on this page comes close: a jump from 13,000 ft, about 60 seconds of freefall at 120 mph, and a pro camera flyer filming the whole thing. It is also non-refundable and only open to adults, so check the weight rules below before you pay.

On a tighter budget, parasailing next to the Burj Al Arab costs about €51 for a 10-minute flight, and a 2-seat jet car you drive yourself works out at roughly €50–62 per person when two share it. Dinner in the Sky (€198) is the pick for someone who wants the height and the views without the fall.

What actually decides it: how much real adrenaline you want per euro, the age and weight limits (the skydive is 18+ with strict weight caps) and whether photos and video are included or sold on site. Below are the 6 options side by side, with filters for budget, kids and couples.

🏆 #1 Best Overall
Skydive Dubai • ⏱️ 3–4 hours on site • 📍 Skydive Dubai, Dubai Marina

Dubai: Skydive Experience at The Palm with Photos & Video

4.8(2k+)
From €679
▾

Pros:

  • The highest, fastest thrill on this page: 13,000 ft and about 60 s of freefall
  • Photos and an edited video by a camera flyer are included
  • Lands on the Palm drop zone with views over Palm Jumeirah and the dunes
  • Small group limited to 2 participants

Cons:

  • €679 per person, the most expensive thrill here by far
  • Non-refundable once booked
  • Strict weight and BMI rules, checked on the day
  • Passport name must match exactly: AED 300 per name change
📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the big one, and it earns its price. You climb for 20 minutes, leave the plane at 13,000 ft strapped to a tandem instructor, and fall for close to a minute before the canopy opens around 6,000 ft. The last 4–5 minutes are a slow ride down with the Palm Jumeirah laid out under you.

The money buys more than the jump. A second skydiver, the camera flyer, films you from exit to landing and grabs a reaction interview on the ground. Photos and a professionally edited video are in the price, which matters because at most activities the media package is the upsell. Per minute in the air (roughly 5–6 minutes) it is about €115–135, so this is a once-in-a-trip splurge, not a casual add-on.

The catches are all about eligibility. You must be 18 on the day, and the weight limits are stricter than most people expect: 90 kg for women and 100 kg for men, including clothes and shoes, with BMI caps near the top. Over 70, or with a list of medical conditions, you need a doctor's certificate. Book with your full passport name in English characters: a name change costs AED 300 at check-in. And plan half a day, not an hour: the full experience runs 3–4 hours.

If you meet the rules, this is the clear #1. If you do not, parasailing (#2 or #5) or Dinner in the Sky (#4) gives you height over the same coastline with far looser limits.

  • Meeting point: Skydive Dubai, Al Seyahi St, Dubai Marina
  • Included: Tandem skydive, photos, professionally edited video, The Palm drop zone
  • Requirements: 18+, women max 90 kg, men max 100 kg (BMI rules near the top), doctor's certificate if over 70 or with listed conditions
  • What to bring: Passport or ID, sports shoes, comfortable sportswear. No sandals, heels, boots, sleeveless shirts or swimwear
  • Languages: English
  • Cancellation: Non-refundable

Cons:

  • If the wind turns, the flight moves near the beach instead of the Burj

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

For the money, nothing on this list beats it. You pay about €51, spend roughly an hour on the boat, and get a 10-minute flight 50 m above the water with the Burj Al Arab right in front of you. The operator's marina sits next to the hotel, so the boat only needs about 5 minutes to reach the spot.

Compared with the Seawake flight (#5), this one is lower (50 m versus about 500 ft) and shorter on the line, but it costs €35 less and gives you the more iconic backdrop. The take-off and landing happen from a purpose-built deck, so non-swimmers and nervous first-timers do not start in the sea. Expect splashes, and possibly a wet landing if the wind is up.

Read the extras carefully. Despite the name, photos are not in the base price: the GoPro photo and video package is optional at check-in, for an extra charge. Budget for that if you want footage. You also need your physical passport or Emirates ID on the day, and check-in is 20 minutes before departure.

It is the best value pick for couples (fly as a duo), families with kids aged 6+ and anyone who wants a taste of height before deciding whether a skydive is worth €679.

  • Optional extra: GoPro photo and video package, chosen at check-in
  • Requirements: 6+ (duo flights for children), solo minimum 45 kg, maximum 150 kg, not for pregnant women

  • Price covers 2 people: about €62 each

  • Burj Al Arab, the Palm and Sheikh Island in one ride
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Cons:

  • Solo travellers pay the same €124
  • 30 minutes on the water goes fast
  • Not for children under 5 or people with high blood pressure
  • Bring your own swimwear and towel
📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

A jet car is a jet ski dressed as a sports car: you sit side by side, steer with a wheel and choose your own speed. This one runs from Umm Suqeim right by the Burj Al Arab, so the hotel is your first stop within minutes, followed by Jumeirah Beach Hotel and a fast stretch along the Palm and past Sheikh Island.

It is priced per car, not per person, and that makes it the best shared thrill on the list. Two people pay €124 together, so about €62 each for 30 minutes, with the instructor's photos and videos in the price. That is cheaper per head than the Seawake parasail and you get two to three times as long on the water.

Against the other jet car (#6), this one costs €24 more but includes the media, while the other operator's edited photos and videos are only sold on site. You also get ice cream, lockers, showers and Wi-Fi. Bring a towel and swimwear, carry ID and the booking voucher, and arrive 15 minutes early for the briefing.

Adrenaline-wise it sits below parasailing for height but above it for control: you decide how hard to push. For a couple who want one shared memory for a fair price, it is the pick.

  • Included: 2-seater jet car ride, instructor, photos and videos, drinking water, ice cream, locker, changing room with open shower, Wi-Fi and charging, sea-view seating
  • Requirements: Not for children under 5, pregnant women or people with high blood pressure
  • What to bring: Passport or ID, swimwear, towel. No luggage or large bags
  • Languages: Arabic, English, Hindi, French, Swahili
  • Cancellation: Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

This is the height without the fall. A crane lifts a shared table 50 m above Dubai Marina, the chefs cook a 3-course meal in front of you, and the platform turns slowly so everyone gets the Palm, the Marina towers and the Burj Al Arab. It runs from the same Al Seyahi Street address as Skydive Dubai, so it pairs neatly with watching a friend land.

The menu is a set three courses with a choice of main: Angus beef short rib, sous-vide chicken, vegetarian tortellini or vegan penne. Juice, soft drinks, coffee and tea are included, alcohol is not.

The rush is mild. We rate it 2 out of 5: if you are nervous about heights the first lift is the moment, after that it is a dinner with a view. It suits mixed groups, birthdays and families with kids aged 8+ who are at least 1.2 m tall. Send your meal choice at least 24 hours ahead and bring your own camera.

If you want the stomach-drop, book the skydive or a parasail. If you want the story and the photos without the fear, this is the one.

Skydiving in Dubai: Price, Limits and What Happens on the Day

The tandem skydive over the Palm is the headline thrill on this page. Here is what the €679 jump involves.

  • Price: from €679 per person, with photos and a professionally edited video included. The booking is non-refundable.
  • Who can do it: 18 or older on the day. Women up to 90 kg (above 85 kg the BMI must be 27.5 or lower), men up to 100 kg (above 95 kg the BMI must be 30 or lower), weighed in clothes and sports shoes. Over the limit and you will not jump. The general suitability note also flags guests over 91 kg, and the booking is non-refundable, so if you weigh between 91 and 100 kg confirm with the operator before paying. Pregnant women and people with respiratory issues cannot take part.
  • Medical: a doctor's certificate is required if you are over 70 or have conditions such as epilepsy, high blood pressure, heart or lung disease, diabetes, a dislocated shoulder or a history of blackouts.
  • The jump: a 20-minute climb, exit at 13,000 ft (4,000 m), nearly 60 seconds of freefall at about 120 mph, parachute open at around 6,000 ft, then 4–5 minutes under canopy over the dunes and Palm Jumeirah.
  • Video and photos: a camera flyer jumps with you and films from exit to landing, including a short reaction interview on the ground.
  • Time: plan 3–4 hours. Briefing, body-position training and harness checks happen about an hour before boarding. Friends can watch from below, and there is a café on site.
  • Paperwork: give your full passport name in English characters when booking. Name changes cost AED 300 per name at check-in.

If the limits rule you out, parasailing at 500 ft or Dinner in the Sky gets you up over the same coastline with much looser rules.

Which One Should You Book?

  • You want the biggest rush and can spend €679: the skydive over the Palm. Nothing else is close.
  • You want real height for under €60: parasailing by the Burj Al Arab, €51 for a 10-minute flight.
  • You are a couple sharing one thrill: the jet car tour, €124 for both of you with photos and videos included. If budget is tighter, the other jet car starts at €100 per car.
  • You are travelling with kids: both parasails accept children from 6 (on duo or group flights), the jet cars from 5, and Dinner in the Sky from 8.
  • Two or three of you want to fly together: the SeaWake parasail, which lifts up to 3 people to about 500 ft.
  • Not everyone in your group wants adrenaline: Dinner in the Sky, a 3-course meal 50 m up, at the same Al Seyahi Street address as Skydive Dubai.

An easy combination for one day: a morning parasail or jet car at Umm Suqeim, then an evening in the dunes (see our desert evening tours compared).

Practical Tips: What to Bring, Timing and Hidden Costs

  • Bring ID. The skydive, both parasails and both jet cars ask for a passport or ID card. The Burj Al Arab parasail needs the physical original passport or Emirates ID.
  • Dress for the activity. For the skydive: sports shoes and sportswear, no sandals, heels, boots, sleeveless tops or swimwear. For parasailing and jet cars: swimwear and a towel (the jet car operators do not include them).
  • Arrive early. Check-in is 20 minutes before the Burj Al Arab parasail and 15 minutes before the jet car tour at #3, for registration and the safety briefing. Late arrivals are not guaranteed a slot.
  • Wind decides the water activities. Parasail flight times run 10–15 minutes depending on wind and sea, and the Burj Al Arab flight moves near the beach if the wind turns. Free cancellation up to 24 hours before applies to everything except the skydive.
  • What costs extra: the GoPro package at #2, edited photos and videos at #6 (sold on site), parking at #6 (AED 3 per hour) and alcohol at Dinner in the Sky (not served at all). Skydive name changes cost AED 300.
  • Where they are: the skydive and Dinner in the Sky share the Al Seyahi Street address in Dubai Marina, both jet cars and the Burj Al Arab parasail run from Umm Suqeim near the hotel, and SeaWake leaves from Dubai Harbour Yacht Club. Frequently Asked Questions

How much does skydiving in Dubai cost?

The tandem skydive over the Palm starts at about €679 per person, and that includes digital photos and a professionally edited video shot by a camera flyer. The booking is non-refundable, so be sure of your date and that you meet the weight limits before paying.

What are the weight and age limits for skydiving in Dubai?

You must be 18 or older on the day. Women can weigh up to 90 kg and men up to 100 kg, measured in clothes and shoes. Above 85 kg (women) or 95 kg (men), a BMI cap of 27.5 or 30 applies. Guests over 91 kg are also flagged as not suitable in the general terms, so confirm with the operator before paying if you are between 91 and 100 kg, because the booking is non-refundable. Over 70, or with conditions like epilepsy, diabetes or high blood pressure, you need a doctor's certificate.

How long does a Dubai skydive take?

The jump itself is a 20-minute climb, about 60 seconds of freefall and a 4–5 minute parachute ride. With briefing, harness fitting and landing, plan 3–4 hours at the drop zone.

How much is parasailing in Dubai?

From about €51 per person for a 10-minute flight at 50 m in front of the Burj Al Arab, or about €86 per person for a 10–15 minute flight at around 500 ft over the Palm, where up to 3 people can fly together. Both accept children from 6.

Is Dinner in the Sky in Dubai worth it?

At about €198 for 90 minutes 50 m up with a 3-course meal cooked live, it is worth it as an experience, not as a thrill. No alcohol is served, the table is shared and the minimum age is 8. If you want adrenaline, a parasail costs a quarter of the price.

How much does a jet car cost in Dubai, and can two people share it?

Jet cars are priced per car for up to 2 people: from €100 for a 20-minute ride or €124 for a 30-minute tour with photos and videos included. Shared, that is about €50–62 each. Children under 5 cannot ride. For classic jet skis, see our jet ski comparison.

What is the most thrilling thing to do in Dubai besides skydiving?

Parasailing gives the most height for the money, and a jet car gives the most speed you control. For a different kind of rush, look at helicopter flights and dune buggies, which we compare on their own pages.
