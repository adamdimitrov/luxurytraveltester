7 Best Kayaking Experiences in Dubai in 2026 (Clear Kayaks, Night Paddles and Hatta Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
7 kayak experiences compared on price, paddle time, location, age limits and pickup
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most visitors the best kayaking in Dubai is the 1-hour clear kayak in Jumeirah at €32 per person: a see-through kayak on the lagoon by Jumeirah Bay Island with the Burj Khalifa on the skyline, and the most-reviewed option on this page. Want mountains instead of skyscrapers? The Hatta Dam day trip (€56) gives you an hour on the reservoir plus a full day of sightseeing, with pickup available.

Three things decide the booking. Age: every city night paddle and the clear kayak are 12+ only, so families with younger kids should look at the Palm (all ages), Hatta (kayak from 6) or the dolphin kayak at Atlantis (8+). Day or night: the illuminated night kayaks are priced per kayak for two, from €39 per person at Dubai Creek Harbour. Transport: only the two Hatta trips come with a driver; every city paddle is make-your-own-way.

Below are 7 kayak experiences, filterable by clear kayak, night paddle, skyline view, Hatta and kid-friendly, with what each price really buys.

Practical Tips: Timing, What to Bring and What Costs Extra

Day or night

The daytime clear kayak's listing tags it for sunrise and sunset, the softest light for the skyline photo. The two night paddles are lit kayaks priced per kayak for up to 2, so they are best value as a couple and expensive solo. Arrive 20 to 30 minutes early for the Jumeirah night kayak; at Creek Harbour there are no walk-ins and late arrivals are not compensated.

Age limits catch families out

The clear kayak and both night paddles are 12+ only. With younger children your options are Dukes The Palm (all ages), Hatta (kayak from 6, infants 0 to 3 free on a lap on the sightseeing tour) and the dolphin kayak at Atlantis (8+, with a paying adult paddling alongside under-12s).

What to bring

For every water option: a change of clothes. Bring swimwear for Dukes The Palm and Atlantis, and biodegradable sunscreen at Dukes. Shoes are not worn on the Jumeirah night kayaks, and sandals or flip-flops are not allowed on the clear kayak. For Hatta add comfortable walking shoes, a sun hat and water. At Atlantis bring a passport or ID card and leave cameras and jewellery behind.

Getting there and what costs extra

Only the two Hatta trips include transport; Hatta is about 1.5 hours each way by road. The city paddles in Jumeirah, the Palm and Creek Harbour are make-your-own-way. Watch for extras: on the #2 Hatta tour, check whether the kayak ticket and lunch are in your option; on the #5 Hatta tour, extra kayak time and quad biking are paid; at Atlantis the dolphin photos are not included.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best kayaking in Dubai?

For most visitors, the 1-hour clear kayak on the Jumeirah Bay lagoon at €32 per person: a transparent kayak with the Burj Khalifa on the skyline. For a full day out, the Hatta Dam kayaking tour from €56 adds mountains and sightseeing with pickup available.

How much does clear kayaking in Dubai cost?

The daytime clear kayak with Burj Khalifa views costs from €32 per person for 1 hour. The illuminated clear kayak at night costs €91 per kayak for up to 2 people, about €46 each.

Can you kayak at night in Dubai?

Yes. There are two night paddles: a lit-up transparent kayak on the Jumeirah Bay lagoon (€91 per kayak for 2) and a neon-lit kayak at Dubai Creek Harbour (€78 per kayak for 2). Both are 1 hour, guided, with Burj Khalifa views and for ages 12+.

Is Hatta kayaking worth the trip from Dubai?

Yes if you have a full day: Hatta Dam sits among rocky mountains about 1.5 hours from Dubai, and the tours combine 1 hour of kayaking with sightseeing or a hill walk. The 7-hour tour at €66 includes hotel pickup, the kayak ticket and lunch.

Can children go kayaking in Dubai?

Yes, but check the age limit. The clear kayak and both night paddles are 12+. Dukes The Palm has no age limit listed, Hatta kayaking starts at 6, and the dolphin kayak at Atlantis is 8+ with a paying adult for under-12s.

Do I need kayaking experience?

No. The guided options start with a safety briefing, Dukes The Palm is set up for first-timers, and the night paddles are on calm water. On the Hatta sightseeing tour, guests with no experience use a water bicycle instead of a kayak.

Is there mangrove kayaking in this comparison?

No. None of the 7 bookable options compared here is a mangrove paddle; they cover the Jumeirah Bay lagoon, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Creek Harbour, Atlantis and Hatta Dam.
