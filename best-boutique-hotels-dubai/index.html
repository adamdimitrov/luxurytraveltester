7 Best Boutique Hotels in Dubai in 2026 (Prices Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
7 boutique, heritage and design hotels in Dubai compared on nightly price, guest score, room size, pool and breakfast
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For a first boutique stay in Dubai, book Al Seef Heritage Hotel at €92 a night: 10 traditional Arabian houses on Dubai Creek with a rooftop infinity pool, and breakfast with free cancellation from €109. Art lovers should pick XVA Art Hotel (€105) in the Al Fahidi lanes, the most space for the money is Canopy by Hilton Al Seef (€95 for 35 m²), the small beach resort is Nikki Beach (€382 with breakfast), and the splurge is Bvlgari Resort (€1,363), an island resort with only 101 rooms and 20 villas.

Most "boutique" lists for Dubai fill up with big chain towers. These seven made the cut because they are heritage houses, art-filled, design-led or genuinely small, and all were compared on price for the same night, guest and location score, room size, pool and breakfast. 📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

Al Seef Heritage is the boutique hotel to book for most first visits. For €92 a night you sleep in one of 10 Bayts, traditional Arabian houses built with Middle Eastern techniques along Dubai Creek, a few steps from the Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood and a short crossing from the spice and gold souks.

What lifts it above the other heritage pick, XVA (#2), is the hardware: a heated rooftop infinity pool with a pool bar, rooms of 25–28 m² instead of 15 m², free parking and a breakfast rate that is hard to beat. €109 gets you breakfast, free cancellation and pay-at-the-property, and guests rate that breakfast superb.

The hotel leans into its setting: Arabic coffee at check-in, Emirati and Arabic Meshawi dishes at Sabaa, and room-service food delivered in picnic baskets. Location scores 9.3, staff 9.3.

The catches: there are no extra beds, only free cots for under-2s, and a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and the AED 15 tourism fee per night come on top of the €92. Couples get the most from it.

  • Check-in: From 15:00, check-out until 12:00. Photo ID and credit card at check-in
  • Breakfast: Included on rates from €109 a night (free cancellation, pay at the property)
  • Rooms: King Heritage Room 269 ft² (about 25 m²); Twin 280 ft²; mobility-accessible Queen 301 ft²
  • Pool: Heated rooftop infinity pool with pool bar
  • Getting there: Al Seef Street on Dubai Creek; Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood a few steps away, the spice and gold souks across the Creek
  • Airport: Dubai International 9 km
  • Parking: Free on-site parking garage with electric charging
  • Dining: Sabaa restaurant (Middle Eastern), open for breakfast and dinner; room service
  • Policies: No minimum check-in age; children 13+ pay as adults; free cots 0–2, no extra beds

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

XVA is the closest thing Dubai has to a classic boutique hotel: a courtyard house in historic Al Bastakiya, with every room individually decorated and art by designers and artists from the region, including Zayan Ghandour and Numa. There is a gallery of international artists and a store with local artwork, crafts and jewellery on site.

For €105 a night you get a 15 m² Superior Double with a patio onto the inner courtyard, and some rooms look out on wind towers and minarets. The award-winning courtyard café is vegetarian, and breakfast can come to your room.

Against Al Seef Heritage (#1), XVA wins on character and service (staff 9.6, overall 9.2) but loses on space and facilities: no pool, rooms about 10 m² smaller and an 11:00 check-out. The Gold Souk and the abra boats are 10 minutes' walk, the Dubai Museum 5.

Small print: the minimum check-in age is 21, extra beds cost AED 275 a night, and a service charge, VAT, the municipality fee and a tourism fee come on top of the €105.

Staying in a Dubai Boutique Hotel: Area, Getting Around and Extra Costs

Which area

  • Al Fahidi and Al Seef (old Dubai): XVA, Al Seef Heritage and Canopy. The only area with real heritage buildings, location scores 9.2–9.3, and Dubai International 9 km from Al Seef. The Gold Souk and the abra boats are about 10 minutes' walk from XVA.
  • Trade Centre and The Greens: 25hours and Zabeel House. Modern districts with design hotels, closer to Downtown (25hours) or to JBR and Mall of the Emirates, both about 4 km from Zabeel House.
  • Jumeira Bay Island: Bvlgari, joined to the Jumeirah coast by a 300 m bridge, about 4 km from Burj Khalifa.
  • Pearl Jumeira Island: Nikki Beach, on the coast close to Downtown and 15 minutes from Dubai International.

Getting around

From old Dubai, Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall are about 10 km by taxi from Al Seef. Staying in Al Fahidi pairs well with an old Dubai and souk tour or a Dubai city tour, and since Al Seef sits on Dubai Creek, a dinner cruise makes an easy first evening. Bvlgari is 4.2 km from the Burj Khalifa.

What costs extra

  • Taxes: a 10% service charge, 5% VAT and a 7% municipality fee come on top of the prices above at most hotels here.
  • Tourism fee: AED 15 per room per night at Al Seef Heritage, Canopy and Zabeel House, AED 20 at 25hours and Nikki Beach.
  • Breakfast: €19 at Canopy, €22 at Zabeel House, €30 on the 25hours flexible rate; included from €109 at Al Seef Heritage and on every Nikki Beach rate shown.
  • Extra beds: from AED 100 (Zabeel House) to AED 300 (Nikki Beach, 13+) a night; free at Bvlgari and for ages 4–5 at Nikki Beach; not available at Al Seef Heritage.

Best months

Prices here are for mid-January, peak winter season in Dubai, so book early for December to March dates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best boutique hotel in Dubai?

Al Seef Heritage Hotel, from €92 a night. It is spread across 10 traditional Arabian houses on Dubai Creek, has a heated rooftop infinity pool and offers breakfast with free cancellation from €109. For art and character, XVA Art Hotel in Al Fahidi (€105) has the higher guest score, 9.2.

Are there heritage hotels in old Dubai?

Yes. XVA Art Hotel is a courtyard house in historic Al Bastakiya (Al Fahidi), and Al Seef Heritage Hotel is built across 10 traditional Bayts on the Al Seef waterfront. Both are walking distance from the souks; pair them with an old Dubai souk tour.

How much does a boutique hotel in Dubai cost per night?

On a January weeknight for two adults, the seven here ran from €92 (Al Seef Heritage) to €1,363 (Bvlgari Resort). Without the two beach resorts (Nikki Beach €382, Bvlgari €1,363) the average was about €138. A service charge, VAT, a municipality fee and a tourism fee are added on top.

Is there a small luxury hotel in Dubai?

Two on this list. Bvlgari Resort Dubai has only 101 rooms and suites plus 20 villas on Jumeira Bay Island, with a private beach, its own marina and a 1,700 m² spa; it scores 9.5, the highest of the seven, from €1,363 a night. Nikki Beach Resort & Spa has 89 rooms, 29 suites and 15 villas on Pearl Jumeira with 450 m of private beach, from €382 with breakfast. For more top-end options, see the best luxury hotels in Dubai.

Which boutique hotels in Dubai allow pets?

Zabeel House The Greens and 25hours Hotel One Central accept pets; charges may apply. XVA, Al Seef Heritage, Canopy, Bvlgari and Nikki Beach do not.

Which Dubai boutique hotel is best for families?

Bvlgari Resort, if the budget allows: cots and extra beds are free for every age. Nikki Beach (€382) has a free Kids' Playroom, free cots up to 3 and free extra beds for 4–5. On a normal budget, Canopy by Hilton Al Seef has 35 m² rooms for €95 and extra beds for AED 105. Avoid Al Seef Heritage (no extra beds) and XVA (minimum check-in age 21).
