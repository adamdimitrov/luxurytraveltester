10 Best Hotels in Downtown Dubai in 2026 (Near Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Downtown hotels compared on price, guest score, walk to Dubai Mall, metro, pools and room size
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most couples the best hotel in Downtown Dubai is Kempinski The Boulevard at €459 a night: the top guest score of the 10 (9.4), a balcony in every room and two pools, a short walk from Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall. Want the fountain from your window? Palace Downtown sells Fountain View rooms from €576. On a budget, Hotel Boulevard scores 9.3 for €222, and families get a 75 m² suite with a kitchen at Wyndham for €139.

All 10 are compared below on the nightly price for the same date, guest score, location score, walk to Dubai Mall and the metro, pools, breakfast and room size. Use the filters for 5-star icons, suites with kitchens, families, rooms under €250 or metro access, or jump to the comparison table.

Staying in Downtown Dubai: Where, Getting Around and What Costs Extra

Which part of Downtown

Mohammed Bin Rashid Boulevard loops around Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall. Kempinski, Address Sky View, DAMAC Maison Mall Street and Hotel Boulevard (in the Old Town section) are on it. Palace Downtown and Address Dubai Mall sit closest to the fountain and the mall. Wyndham is at the Dubai Opera end, a 10-minute walk from the mall. Sofitel and Leva are on Sheikh Zayed Road, the metro side: closer to trains, further from the fountain. Leva is across the road, with an Al Wasl / City Walk address.

Getting around

Dubai Mall/Burj Khalifa metro station is beside Sofitel and 5 minutes' walk from Leva; Business Bay station is 7 minutes from Leva. Wyndham runs a free shuttle to the metro, Dubai Mall and Jumeirah Beach Park. Dubai International Airport is listed 13–15 km from these hotels, 15–20 minutes by car. Most hotels here include free parking.

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: the prices above exclude 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and the Dubai tourism fee of AED 20 per room per night at the 5-stars here (AED 15 at Hotel Boulevard).
  • Breakfast: €25 (Sofitel), €30 (EDITION), €31 (Hotel Boulevard), €41 (Address Sky View) on room-only rates.
  • Deposits: AED 1,000 on a credit card at DAMAC Maison Mall Street, AED 300 in cash at Wyndham.
  • Extra beds: AED 150–350 a night where offered; Hotel Boulevard, Palace and Address Sky View have none.

Best months

The prices here are for mid-January, the cool, high season when evenings by the fountain are pleasant. Downtown is also the base for most sightseeing: the tower is next door (see our Burj Khalifa tickets comparison), and many Dubai city tours and desert safaris pick up from Downtown hotels.

For a half day in the older part of the city, the Old Dubai souk and abra tours compare the guided walks around Al Fahidi, the Creek and the souks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best hotel in Downtown Dubai?

Kempinski The Boulevard has the highest guest score of the 10 (9.4) and a 9.7 location score, from €459 a night with a balcony room and two pools. For a lower budget, Hotel Boulevard scores 9.3 for €222.

Which Downtown Dubai hotel has the best Burj Khalifa and fountain views?

Palace Downtown overlooks the Dubai Fountain and Burj Lake; its Fountain View rooms start at €576. Address Dubai Mall sells Burj Khalifa and Fountain View rooms with balconies, and Address Sky View has a level 54 infinity pool facing the tower. Always book the view category: the cheapest room usually faces the lake or the city.

Which hotels are connected to Dubai Mall?

Address Dubai Mall connects directly into Dubai Mall by a link bridge. Palace Downtown is a 5-minute walk, Wyndham 10 minutes (or its free shuttle), and Leva 1 km across Sheikh Zayed Road.

What is the cheapest good hotel near Burj Khalifa?

Leva Hotel and Suites at €95, rated 9.0, 1 km from Burj Khalifa and 5 minutes' walk from the metro, though it is across Sheikh Zayed Road rather than in Downtown. Inside Downtown, DAMAC Maison Mall Street (€137, 70 m²) and Wyndham (€139, 75 m² suite) are the cheapest.

Which Downtown Dubai hotel is best for families?

Wyndham Downtown: one-bedroom suites with kitchens from €139, a kids' pool, indoor and outdoor play areas, free cots and a free shuttle to Dubai Mall and Jumeirah Beach Park. DAMAC Maison Mall Street has 2- and 3-bedroom units and a kids' pool.

Is the tourism fee included in Downtown Dubai hotel prices?

No. The nightly prices on this page exclude 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and the Dubai tourism fee (AED 20 per night at the 5-stars here, AED 15 at Hotel Boulevard). They are added at booking.

Is Downtown or Business Bay better for a first trip?

Downtown if you want to walk to Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall and the fountain shows. Business Bay is next door and often cheaper; see our Business Bay hotel comparison. For an evening on the water from either area, compare Dubai dinner cruises.
