Why Atlantis, The Palm Took the #1 Spot

Atlantis The Royal is more luxurious and Anantara is calmer, but Atlantis, The Palm gave me the most for the money. The waterpark and aquarium alone would cost a family hundreds, and the rooms, food and service were all top level. It is the best all-round stay on the Palm, which is why it is my top recommendation.

My video review of Atlantis, The Palm.

1. Aquaventure and The Lost Chambers Are Included

Hotel guests get free entry to Aquaventure World, with over 105 slides and attractions, rapids and a 1 km private beach. I tried it all. It is a blast, but expect plenty of stairs and queues.

The Lost Chambers Aquarium has over 65,000 marine animals in 21 exhibits, with glass tunnels full of sharks and rays. It is open daily from 10 AM to 9 PM and free for resort guests.

2. Big, Comfortable Rooms, Even at Entry Level

I booked the cheapest Palm King room and paid 9,659.79 AED (about €2,432) for 5 nights. The bed was exceptionally comfortable, the room has a Nespresso machine and a marble work desk, and sheets and towels were changed every day.

Pick the Palm view over the Ocean view: you look over the courtyard, Palm Jumeirah and the Dubai skyline, while the sea side has much less to see.

3. The Best Buffets of All the Hotels I Tested

Breakfast runs from 7:00 to 11:30 at Saffron and Kaleidoscope, which serve the same food. Arrive before 9 AM to skip the queue. At dinner, Saffron is Dubai's largest Asian buffet, with over 220 dishes and a different theme every night. Both were the best buffets I had at any hotel on the Palm.

On top of that there are celebrity-chef and Michelin-starred restaurants such as Ossiano, Hakkasan, Nobu and FZN, at extra cost.

4. Service That Went Above and Beyond

Staff took my luggage straight from the Uber to my room, and I was let into the room by 11 AM without asking for early check-in. Housekeeping left small gifts without being asked, a mouse pad and a glasses cloth. Every member of staff I dealt with was friendly and quick to help.

5. Easy for Families

The Family Pool has shallow areas and lifeguards, and the private beach comes with free sun loungers, umbrellas and towels. The Atlantis Explorers Club keeps kids aged 4 to 12 busy with themed zones and workshops, and there is a supervised play area for toddlers, run by staff trained in paediatric first aid.

What I Didn't Love

  • The room doors aren't soundproof. I could hear slamming doors, conversations and children in the hallway, especially in the morning.
  • The Wi-Fi was unstable. Video calls dropped and pages loaded slowly when I tried to work.
  • It is busy. It is the busiest resort on the Palm, and Aquaventure has queues.
  • Breakfast is not always in the rate. If it is not, it costs €42 per person.
  • The top restaurants cost extra. So does the WHITE Beach club.

Quick Questions

Is Aquaventure included?

Yes. Hotel guests get free entry to Aquaventure World and The Lost Chambers Aquarium.

Which room should I book?

The Palm King room with a Palm view. It is the cheapest category and the view over the Palm and the skyline beats the Ocean view.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in from 15:00 and check-out until 12:00, as listed on Booking.com. Members of the free Atlantis Unlocked programme can ask for a late check-out.

Atlantis, The Palm or Atlantis The Royal?

Atlantis, The Palm for value and families, from €300 a night. Atlantis The Royal for a splurge, from €1,000 a night.

Is breakfast included?

It depends on your rate. If it is not included, breakfast costs €42 per person.

My pick for your Palm Jumeirah stay

Atlantis, The Palm in a Palm King room with the Palm view. Get to breakfast before 9 AM and to Aquaventure early.
