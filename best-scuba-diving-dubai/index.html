8 Best Scuba Diving Experiences in Dubai in 2026 (Pool vs Sea, Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
8 Dubai dives compared on certification, pool vs sea, depth, trip time, transfer and price
Last updated: Nov 16, 2026

Never dived? Book the €86 Discover Scuba dive at Jumeirah Beach: 3 hours, down to 12 metres in the open sea, deeper than the €173 private dive that costs twice as much. Already certified? Go east: 2 guided reef dives in Fujairah cost €148 with hotel transfer, €74 a dive, against €296 for a single dive at Deep Dive Dubai.

Two questions decide the rest. Do you have a certificate, and do you want the pool or the open sea? Deep Dive Dubai is the world's deepest pool, 2 hours in the city with a sunken set and no fish. Fujairah on the east coast means coral, wrecks and turtles, but a 6:00 AM pickup, an 8-hour day and on two trips an AED 160 transfer fee that is not in the headline price.

Below are the 8 best options, from €86 sea dives to Deep Dive Dubai's 30-metre guided dive, plus snorkelling picks for non-divers. Use the filters to see only beginner, certified, pool or sea trips.

  • Cheapest scuba option on this page at €86

  • Only 3 hours, fits a beach or city day
  • Diving equipment and instruction included
  • Free cancellation and reserve now, pay later

Cons:

  • Gulf water off a city beach, not the east coast reefs
  • Shared group of up to 10, not private
  • No flying on the same day as your dive
📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the sensible default for anyone who types "scuba diving Dubai" and has never dived. For €86 you get a 3-hour session with skills training, then a guided descent off Jumeirah Beach to a maximum of 12 metres. That depth matters: the pool and private options on this page stop at 4 m (Sunken City Walk) or 6–8 m (VIP private dive), so you get a deeper first dive for less money.

Compared with the Fujairah day trips at €107, you save €21 on the listed price, skip the AED 160 transfer fee those trips add, and avoid a 6:00 AM pickup. The trade-off is the setting: the east coast sells coral gardens and turtles, while this is a city-side dive. If your priority is simply breathing underwater for the first time, this one does it in a morning.

Equipment and instruction are included, and you meet at the centre at Azure Residences on Palm Jumeirah, handy if you are staying nearby. Hotel pickup is not part of the package, so sort out your own taxi. You cannot fly on the same day after diving, so do not book it for your departure day.

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the dive behind most "deep dive Dubai" searches from divers. You get one guided dive in a 60-metre indoor pool with a flooded city to explore, and the depth is set by your card: open-water divers stay shallow, advanced divers can go to 30 metres. Entry, equipment, gases and a dive guide are included, and the whole thing takes about 2 hours. You make your own way to NAS Sports Complex.

At €296 it costs twice the Fujairah 2-dive trip (#4, €148 for two dives, so €74 per dive against €296 here). You are paying for the setting, the visibility and the convenience of not leaving Dubai, not for marine life. Book it for the novelty, and book the east coast if you want fish.

It is non-refundable, so lock it in only once your dates are fixed. Plan the rest of the day too: you must avoid flying or high altitude, Burj Khalifa included, for at least 12 hours after a single dive (18 hours after more than one), so book your Burj Khalifa tickets for an earlier day.

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

Deep Dive Dubai's way in for people who will not swim or dive. You gear up, step onto a platform 4 metres below the surface and explore the sunken city's props with a guide, all within a 30-minute session that includes gearing up, in a group of no more than six. The whole visit runs about 1.5 hours.

The maths is the catch: €148 for a 30-minute session is about €4.90 per minute, and part of that is gearing up, and it is non-refundable. For the same €148 a certified diver gets two sea dives (#4), and for €86 a swimmer gets a 12 m open-water dive (#1). Snorkelling in Dubai: Pool or Sea

If nobody in your group wants a tank on their back, there are two snorkelling routes:

  • Sea snorkelling in Fujairah: both east coast beginner trips (#2 and #5) include snorkelling gear, and #2 specifically snorkels where sea turtles feed. You book the dive trip, so it is a full day, but you get the dive and the snorkel for one price.

Before You Book: Flying, Documents and Extra Costs

  • No flying, no Burj Khalifa straight after. Deep Dive Dubai asks you to avoid flying or high altitude for 12 hours after one dive and 18 hours after more than one, the Fujairah half-day trip says wait 18 hours before flying, and the Jumeirah Beach dive says no flying on the same day. Dive early in your trip and schedule your Burj Khalifa visit helicopter or balloon flights on another day.
  • Passport: the Fujairah trips (#2, #4) ask for your original passport; most other listings ask for a passport or ID card.
  • Certification card: certified-only options (#3, #4) need your licence or a photo of it (Deep Dive Dubai accepts an image).
  • Waivers: all three Deep Dive Dubai options require an online waiver before the visit, and no alcohol or non-prescription drugs for 24 hours before.
  • What costs extra: the AED 160 return transfer on #2 and #5, paid on arrival. Underwater photos are included only on #6; Deep Dive Dubai sells video and facility-tour upgrades.
  • Early starts: Fujairah pickups are between 6:00 and 7:00 AM. You are back by early afternoon, so an evening desert safari fits the same day better than the night before.
  • Bring: swimwear and a towel for every option (towels are supplied on #6).
  • Cancellation: the five sea options have free cancellation up to 24 hours before; the three Deep Dive Dubai tickets are non-refundable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does scuba diving in Dubai cost?

A first dive in the sea starts at €86 per person (3 hours at Jumeirah Beach). East coast beginner trips to Fujairah are €107 plus AED 160 for the return transfer. Certified divers pay €148 for 2 guided dives with transfer in Fujairah, or €296 for one guided dive at Deep Dive Dubai.

Can I scuba dive in Dubai without a certificate?

Yes. The Discover Scuba dive at Jumeirah Beach (€86), the VIP private dive (€173) and both Fujairah beginner trips (€107) need no certificate. The VIP private dive and the Deep Dive Sunken City Walk do not even require you to swim.

How much is Deep Dive Dubai?

For certified divers, a guided dive including entry, equipment and gases costs from €296. Non-divers can do the 30-minute Sunken City Walk for €148 or 1 hour of surface snorkelling for €99. All three are non-refundable.

Is diving better in Dubai or Fujairah?

For marine life, Fujairah. The east coast trips dive coral gardens, wrecks and reefs with turtles and reef fish, but take about 8 hours with a 6:00–7:00 AM pickup. Dubai is better for a short first dive (Jumeirah Beach, 3 hours) or for the Deep Dive Dubai pool.

What is the minimum age for scuba diving in Dubai?

On this shortlist, 10 for Deep Dive Dubai's guided dive and Sunken City Walk, and 12 for the Fujairah half-day dive and snorkel trip. Children from 6 can join the Deep Dive Dubai snorkelling session.

Where can I go snorkeling in Dubai?

At Deep Dive Dubai's pool (€99, 1 hour, ages 6+) or on the Fujairah beginner dive trips, which include snorkelling with reef fish and sea turtles after the dive.

Can I go up the Burj Khalifa after diving?

Not on the same day. Deep Dive Dubai asks you to wait at least 12 hours after a single dive (18 hours after more than one) before flying or going to high altitude, the Burj Khalifa included. Book your Burj Khalifa tickets for a different day.
