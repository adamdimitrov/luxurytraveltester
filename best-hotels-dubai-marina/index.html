10 Best Hotels in Dubai Marina in 2026 (Prices Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Dubai Marina hotels compared on nightly price, guest score, room size, pool, beach and breakfast
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most couples the Dubai Marina hotel to book is Grosvenor House at €236 a night: the highest guest score of the ten, 10 restaurants and free beach access at its sister resort. The best-value 5★ is InterContinental at €162 for a 43 m² room facing Dubai Marina Mall, families should look at JW Marriott (€257, kids' club), and the cheapest hotel is The First Collection at €96.

The catch most visitors miss: only one hotel here, the W at Mina Seyahi, is on the beach. The rest reach the sand on foot or by shuttle. All 10 were compared on price for the same night, guest and location score, pool and beach access, breakfast cost and room size. Staying in Dubai Marina: Where, Getting Around and Extra Costs

Which part of the Marina

  • Marina promenade and Dubai Marina Mall (Grosvenor House, InterContinental, JW Marriott): location scores 9.3–9.6, with the mall on foot (JW Marriott is linked to it directly). Vida and Marriott Harbour score 9.6 and 9.5.
  • Marina Gate and the JBR end (Jumeirah Living, Ciel): towers under 1–2 km from The Walk at JBR; Ciel is 8 minutes' walk from Barasti Beach.
  • Mina Seyahi (W): the Marina's beach edge, the only true beachfront address here, with the lowest location score (8.6).
  • Budget blocks (City Premiere, 750 m from Dubai Marina Metro; The First Collection on Al Seba Street): bigger units for less money; Hidden Beach is a 17-minute walk from The First Collection.

Getting around

Dubai Marina Metro Station is 750 m from City Premiere, and Jumeirah Living has metro, tram and water taxi stations about 1 km away. InterContinental has easy access to the Dubai Tram. Grosvenor House puts Dubai International at about 20 minutes by taxi; most other hotels list Al Maktoum International at 21–27 km. Parking is free at nine of the ten.

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: the room prices above exclude taxes and charges. Where the rates break them down, a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and a Dubai tourism fee of AED 15–20 per room per night are added (AED 30 on Marriott Harbour's two-bedroom suites).
  • Breakfast: €20 (InterContinental), €22 (Jumeirah Living), €29 (Marriott Harbour), €33 (W), €36 (JW Marriott). Only Marriott Harbour includes it at the lowest price.
  • Beach: free at the W (beachfront) and through Grosvenor House's sister resort; The First Collection lists a private beach area and free shuttle. Elsewhere you walk or taxi to a public beach.
  • Extra beds: from AED 100 (InterContinental, Marriott Harbour) to AED 360 (Grosvenor House) a night; free for under-12s at Jumeirah Living; not available at Vida.

When to go

The rates here are for mid-January, the cooler winter season when the pools, rooftop bars and beach clubs are at their best. The Marina is also the starting point for many boat trips: compare Dubai dinner cruises and jet ski tours if you want to see the skyline from the water.

The Marina is also where most boat trips leave from: our Dubai boat tours compared puts 10 speedboat and shared yacht cruises side by side on price per hour, route and departure point.

What is the best hotel in Dubai Marina?

Grosvenor House, a Luxury Collection Hotel, from €236 a night. It has the highest guest score of the ten compared (9.5), 10 restaurants and free beach and pool access at its sister resort Le Royal Meridien with a shuttle. For a beachfront room, pick W Dubai Mina Seyahi (€248).

How much does a hotel in Dubai Marina cost per night?

On a January weeknight for two adults, the ten options here ran from €95 (City Premiere, a one-bedroom apartment) to €257 (JW Marriott). The median was about €214. Taxes, VAT and a Dubai tourism fee of AED 15–20 per room per night come on top.

Do Dubai Marina hotels have a private beach?

JW Marriott Hotel Marina for kids over 2 (kids' club, children's pool, direct link to Dubai Marina Mall), and Jumeirah Living Marina Gate for babies and young children (free cots up to 3, free extra beds up to 11, kids' pool).
