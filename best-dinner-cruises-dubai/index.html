7 Best Dinner Cruises in Dubai in 2026 (Dhow vs Mega-Yacht vs Canal)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
7 Dubai dinner cruises compared on boat type, route, menu, drinks, show, price per hour and transfers
Last updated: Nov 16, 2026

For most people, the right Dubai dinner cruise is the €52 Marina glass boat with an open bar. You get 90 minutes past Ain Dubai, JBR and Dubai Harbour, an international buffet with live pasta and noodle stations, and unlimited beer, wine and spirits in the price. No other dinner cruise on this list includes alcohol in its base fare.

The common mistake is booking a €22 wooden dhow and expecting the same night. The budget dhows are rated almost a full point lower (3.8–3.9 out of 5) and leave alcohol out of the package, and the cheapest one gives dinner a 45-minute slot. They are fine for a cheap Tanoura show on the water, as long as you know that is what you are buying.

If you would rather see the Burj Khalifa than the Marina towers, pick the canal route: a glass-enclosed dhow under the Sheikh Zayed Road waterfall bridge for €51, or €137 with a Gold Pass for La Perle afterwards. Want a seated multi-course dinner instead of a buffet? That is the €104 Michelin-chef menu on a Marina yacht.

Below are 7 dinner cruises side by side on boat type, route, buffet vs set menu, drinks, show, duration, price per hour and transfer. Filter by dhow, mega-yacht, open bar, budget or show included.

Pros:

  • Unlimited beer, wine and spirits included, no bar tab
  • Live pasta and noodle stations on top of the buffet
  • Glass-enclosed lower deck plus open-air upper deck
  • 4.7 rating on the largest review base in this comparison
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before, reserve now and pay later

Cons:

  • Shortest cruise of the mid-price options at 90 minutes
  • No hotel transfer included, you make your own way to the Marina
  • Entertainment is background melodic music, no Tanoura or dance show
📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the benchmark every other cruise on the page gets measured against. €52 buys 90 minutes, a full buffet and an open bar, which works out at about €35 per hour on the water. The canal cruise at #2 costs almost the same but charges extra for alcohol, and the Michelin-menu yacht at #3 sells drinks at the bar on top of €104. If you plan on two or three drinks, the open bar alone covers a large part of the fare.

The boat is a modern two-deck vessel: glass-enclosed below with panoramic views, open-air above. The route stays in the Marina and Dubai Harbour area, so the views are Ain Dubai, the JBR towers and the Marina skyline lit up at night, not Downtown. Food is a continental and oriental buffet with two live cooking stations, which is more than the standard tray buffet on the cheaper dhows.

The trade-off is the mood. Music is soft and romantic rather than a show, so families hoping for a Tanoura dancer should look at #5 or the dhows. And at 90 minutes, it is a dinner, not a full evening. Pair it with something earlier in the day, like a Burj Khalifa slot, and you have the classic Dubai day.

  • Meeting point: Xclusive Yachts boarding point, Dubai Marina
  • Included: 90-minute cruise, international buffet dinner with live stations, unlimited beer, wine and spirits, unlimited soft drinks, juice, water, tea and coffee
  • Return: Back at the dock around 10:00 PM
  • Cancellation: Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

At €51 for 2–2.5 hours it costs about the same as #1 but gives you roughly an extra hour on the water, around €20–25 per hour.

The boat is the hybrid of this page: a dhow in shape, glass-encased so you dine indoors. Boarding starts with Arabic kahwah, dates and chilled refreshments, then a buffet of Arabic appetisers, grilled meat, salads and Arabian sweets with live stations, with Arabic music and a saxophone set.

Two catches. Alcohol is an optional paid add-on, so if you drink, the real price climbs above #1. And entertainment is not available during Ramadan. Hotel pickup and drop-off is an option at booking, which matters here because Festival City is a long way from Marina and JBR hotels. If you also want La Perle, #4 is the same canal dinner with the Gold Pass bundled.

Practical Tips: Routes, Transfers and Hidden Extras

Marina vs Creek vs Canal

Marina and Dubai Harbour (#1, #3, #5, #7) means Ain Dubai, Bluewaters, JBR and the Marina towers lit up, with departures close to Palm and JBR hotels. Dubai Creek (#6) is the old trading waterway and the most traditional setting. Dubai Water Canal (#2, #4) boards at Al Faris Floating Restaurant in Dubai Festival City and is the only route that passes the Burj Khalifa and Business Bay.

Getting there

Transfers decide the real cost. #2, #4 and #6 offer pickup as a booking option, #3 and #5 charge extra (book #5's pickup at least 6 hours ahead), and #1 and #7 include none. Festival City is far from Marina hotels, so take the pickup option on the canal cruises if you are staying at JBR or on the Palm.

What costs extra

  • Alcohol on every cruise except #1 (paid add-on on #2, bar on #3, house beverages by option on #4, not included on #6 and #7, not listed on #5).
  • Souvenir photos on #4 and #5.
  • Confirmed al fresco seating on #4: 50 AED per person.
  • Tips on #7.

Timing and what to bring

#1 is back at the dock around 10:00 PM, #3 sails 8:00–10:00 PM and #4 boards at 6:15 PM for a 7:00 PM departure. Bring a camera and a light layer for the open upper decks. Large bags, strollers and outside alcohol are not allowed on the Dubai Harbour dhow (#7), and #4 asks for smart casual clothing. During Ramadan, check the show: #2 pauses its entertainment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which Dubai dinner cruise includes alcohol?

Only the Marina open-bar cruise (#1) includes unlimited beer, wine and spirits in its €52 fare. The canal cruises charge extra or include house beverages only on a higher option, the Michelin-chef yacht has a paid bar, and the dhows do not include alcohol.

Is a dhow cruise on Dubai Creek or Dubai Marina better?

The Creek is the traditional, old-Dubai setting; the Marina has the modern skyline with Ain Dubai and JBR. Ratings are higher on the Marina's modern boats. If you want the Creek specifically, #6 is the Creek dhow, about €30 with optional hotel pickup.

How much does a dinner cruise in Dubai cost?

From €22 per person for a basic Marina dhow to €52 for a glass boat with an open bar, €104 for a seated Michelin-chef menu and €137 for a canal dinner plus a La Perle Gold Pass.

Which dinner cruise passes the Burj Khalifa?

The Dubai Water Canal cruises (#2 and #4). They sail from Dubai Festival City through Business Bay, pass the Burj Khalifa and go under the Sheikh Zayed Road waterfall bridge. Marina cruises do not see the Burj Khalifa up close.

Are Dubai dinner cruises buffet or set menu?

Almost all are buffets, several with live cooking stations. The exception is #3, a seated multi-course menu created by a Michelin-starred chef, 8:00–10:00 PM from Dubai Harbour.

Which dinner cruise is best for kids?

#5, the Marina catamaran: Tanura dance, live music, a DJ and a Darbuka show, unlimited soft drinks, a choice of air-conditioned or open deck and wheelchair access. Note that #4 is not suitable for children under 2.

Can I cancel a Dubai dinner cruise?

All 7 cruises here offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before, and you can reserve now and pay later.
