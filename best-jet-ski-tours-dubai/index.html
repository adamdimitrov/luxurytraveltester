7 Best Jet Ski Tours in Dubai in 2026 (Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
7 guided jet ski tours compared on minutes, route, € per minute, riders per ski and photos
Last updated: Nov 16, 2026

For a first jet ski ride past the Burj Al Arab, book the Yoush Water Sports 30-minute tour: €33 per jet ski, two riders can share it, and photos, videos and an ice cream are included. That works out at about €1.11 per minute, or roughly €17 each for a couple.

The catch most people miss: nearly every tour has "Burj Al Arab & Atlantis" in its title, but the cheapest 30-minute rides from Umm Suqeim (Yoush, Nemo, Sun and Fun) list only the Burj Al Arab stretch. If Atlantis and the Palm matter, book a longer option that actually routes past them, or start from Dubai Marina.

Below, 7 different tours are compared on what actually changes the booking: minutes on the water, route, price per jet ski vs per person, riders per ski, photo and video, departure marina and age limits. Filter by budget, longer rides or the Palm route, then check the side-by-side table under the cards.

  • Lockers, changing room, shower and Wi-Fi at the base

Cons:

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the default booking for a Burj Al Arab jet ski ride. For €33.24 you get one jet ski for 30 minutes, and that price covers one or two riders. Split between two people it comes to about €16.60 each, which nobody else on this page beats once you account for the time on the water.

The route is the short, photogenic one: a safety briefing at the P&O Marina Port fishing harbour in Umm Suqeim 2, then out to the Burj Al Arab, past Jumeirah Beach Hotel and back with the Burj Khalifa skyline in view. The instructor takes the photos and videos for free, and you also get ice cream, water and access to lockers, a changing room and an open shower.

Compared with Nemo (#2), the two are almost the same product from the same harbour at the same price. Yoush edges it on rating and per-minute cost, and it lets you swap drivers mid-ride. Nemo's extra is a café stop with ice cream or pizza. If you want Atlantis or the Palm, look at the coastline tour (#3), Shark from Dubai Marina (#4) or Sun and Fun's 60-minute option (#7), whose routes include them.

Shark leaves from Dubai Marina, which puts Ain Dubai on Bluewaters Island a short ride away, then JBR Beach, the Palm and Atlantis. Longer options add the Burj Al Arab.

The trade-off is price. The from-price is €88.64 for one rider on a private tour, which is about €2.95 a minute if it is the 30-minute option, more than double the Umm Suqeim rides. Two practical catches: drivers must be at least 18 (under-18s can only ride as passengers with an adult), and the marina does not allow showers or changing rooms, so come in swimwear. Practical Tips: Licence, Age, What to Bring and Getting There

Do you need a licence?

None of these 7 tours asks for a boat or jet ski licence. You get a safety briefing, then follow a guide on your own jet ski. What you do need is ID: every listing asks for a passport or ID card, and Sun and Fun wants an original passport or Emirates ID at registration.

Age limits

Most operators let you drive from 16 (Yoush, A One, Sun and Fun, the coastline tour). Shark in Dubai Marina requires drivers to be 18, with under-18s riding as passengers next to an adult. Passengers can be as young as 4 or 5 depending on the operator. The coastline tour has a 136 kg (300 lbs) limit, and A One is not suitable for people with back problems.

Getting there

Six of the seven start next to the Burj Al Arab, five of them from the fishing harbours of Umm Suqeim 2 on Jumeirah Street. Nemo has two public car parks on site and Friends Aqua includes parking. Shark is the exception, inside Dubai Marina. Plan your own transport: hotel pickup is not included on Nemo, A One, Sun and Fun or Friends Aqua.

What to bring and what costs extra

  • Swimwear and a towel (Sun and Fun and Friends Aqua do not provide towels).
  • Passport or Emirates ID.
  • Arrive 15–20 minutes early for registration and the briefing.
  • Photos and videos are free on most tours, but Nemo asks you to collect them before you leave the base.
  • Tips are optional and not included at Shark and Sun and Fun.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a jet ski tour in Dubai cost?

A guided 30-minute jet ski ride past the Burj Al Arab starts at about €33–36 per jet ski, which covers one or two riders on most tours. A 45-minute ride costs about €69 per ski, and a 1.5–2 hour coastline tour about €131 per ski. Friends Aqua charges per person, from €39.49.

Can you see Atlantis on a 30-minute jet ski tour?

Not on the 30-minute rides compared here. Yoush and Nemo list only the Burj Al Arab and Jumeirah Beach Hotel stretch, and Sun and Fun keeps the Royal Atlantis for its 60-minute option. The A One 45-minute ride names Atlantis and offers an optional Ain Dubai stop beyond the Palm, so confirm its route at check-in. Starting from Dubai Marina with Shark gets you to the Palm and Atlantis fastest.

Can two people share one jet ski?

Yes. Every tour here uses two-seat jet skis. Yoush, Nemo, A One and the coastline tour charge per jet ski, so two people pay one price. On Yoush you can also swap drivers during the ride. Friends Aqua charges per person instead.

Do I need a licence to ride a jet ski in Dubai?

Not on these guided tours. You get a safety briefing and follow your guide. Bring a passport or Emirates ID, and check the driver age: 16+ on most tours, 18+ at Shark in Dubai Marina.

Are photos and videos included?

On most of them, yes. Yoush, Nemo, A One, Friends Aqua and Sun and Fun include photos and videos taken by the guide. The coastline tour includes photos, and Shark offers free photos on a mobile phone.

Where do Dubai jet ski tours start?

Six of the seven start next to the Burj Al Arab, five of them from the fishing harbours in Umm Suqeim 2. Shark starts in Dubai Marina, which is closer to Ain Dubai, JBR and the Palm. If you are staying on the Palm, see our Palm Jumeirah hotels guide.

What is the minimum age for a jet ski tour in Dubai?

Drivers must be 16 on most tours and 18 at Shark. Passengers can join from 4 or 5 years old depending on the operator; Friends Aqua and Yoush do not take children under 5.
