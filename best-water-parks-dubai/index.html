7 Best Water Parks in Dubai in 2026 (Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
7 water park tickets compared on price, slides, travel time and on-site extras
Last updated: Nov 16, 2026

For most people the answer is the Aquaventure World day pass at €77: 105+ slides, a private beach and big kids zones on Palm Jumeirah, which works out to about €0.73 per slide. Add The Lost World Aquarium for about €5 more if you have kids, or go to LEGOLAND Water Park (€73) if your children are under 10.

What catches people out is not the ticket. At Atlantis, towels and lockers add AED 115 before you buy lunch, LEGOLAND Water Park is shut on Wednesdays, and Yas Waterworld, the other big name, is not in Dubai at all: it is in Abu Dhabi, more than an hour from Dubai by road, with no transport in the ticket.

Below are the 7 tickets worth booking, from the plain day pass to the dolphin package and the multi-park passes, compared on price, slides, travel time and what you pay once inside. Filter by thrills, kids or day trips to narrow it down.

🏆 #1 Best Overall
Atlantis Aquaventure World • ⏱️ Full day • 📍 Atlantis, The Palm

Atlantis Aquaventure World Waterpark Day Pass

4.5(14.2k+)
From €77
▾

Pros:

  • 105+ slides, the most of any park on this page
  • Private beach and lazy river included
  • Dedicated Splashers zones for kids
  • Child rate for ages 3 to 6.99 under 1.2 m, under 2s free
  • Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Cons:

  • Towels (from AED 35) and lockers (from AED 80) cost extra
  • AquaXpress fast pass is extra, AED 225–795 on-site
  • No outside food or drinks
  • Food and drinks are bought on site
📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the default answer for most people searching for a Dubai water park. For €77 you get a full day on 105+ slides, which works out to roughly €0.73 per slide, against about €1.60 at Yas Waterworld and €3.60 at Legoland Water Park. The headline ride is the Leap of Faith, a near-vertical drop through a tunnel in a shark lagoon, and there is a lazy river, the Surf's Up wave rider and a private beach for the hours in between.

The ticket is cheap; the day is not. Towels start at AED 35 and lockers at AED 80, outside food is not allowed, so lunch and drinks are bought inside the park. Budget for those, and bring your own towel and water shoes to cut the bill.

Families get a real kids area here, not a token splash pad: the Splashers zones cover four areas. Pricing is by height, so a child under 1.2 m aged 3 to 6.99 pays the child rate and under 2s go free with ID. If your group wants the Lost World Aquarium as well, do not buy this ticket: the combo at #2 adds it for about €5.

  • Location: Aquaventure Waterpark, Atlantis The Palm, Crescent Rd, Palm Jumeirah
  • Hours: Daily 09:15 until sunset
  • Included: Full-day pass, 105+ slides, private beach, Surf's Up wave rider, Splashers kids zones
  • Not included: Towel (from AED 35) and locker (from AED 80) rental, AquaXpress fast pass (AED 225–795), meals
  • What to bring: Swimwear, towel
  • Rules: Under-12s need an adult; no thongs, transparent swimwear, denim or loose clothing such as abayas in the water
  • Cancellation: Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

The cheapest upgrade on this page. The combo costs €81 against €77 for the waterpark alone, so The Lost World Aquarium comes in at roughly €5. You get the exact same waterpark day as the #1 pick, plus general entry to an aquarium with 65,000 marine animals, 14 chambers and mermaid performances.

The real win is the timing. The waterpark closes at sunset, the aquarium stays open until 21:00. That means the aquarium does not eat into slide time: you leave the park, dry off and walk through the chambers in the evening, which is ideal with kids who need a slower last hour.

Why is it #2 and not #1? Because the waterpark is what people come for and the plain pass is simpler. The combo also lists more restrictions (not suitable over 113 kg or with back, heart or mobility problems) and each attraction is one entry only. If those do not apply to you, this is the better buy.

This is the premium Atlantis day. At €216 it costs about €139 more than the waterpark alone, and what that buys is a supervised Shallow Water Interaction: 30 minutes of briefing and changing, then 30 minutes in waist-deep water with the resident dolphins in Dolphin Bay. The waterpark pass is included for the same day.

Read the small print before booking. This does not include swimming with the dolphins; you stand in the shallows while the trainers run enrichment and training sessions. Wetsuits, water, juices and coffee are included, and towels and lockers are provided, but only for the encounter. For the slides afterwards you are back to renting or bringing your own.

It earns its place for families: children 2 and under join free, all ages can take part, and it is the best-rated product here. For couples or thrill seekers who mostly want slides, the €77 pass and a separate treat elsewhere is the better use of the money.

  • Not included: Photos (sold at the Dolphin Bay shop), meals
  • Languages: English
  • Kids: 2 and under free; under-12s must be with a paying adult
  • Cancellation: Free cancellation up to 24 hours before

Practical Tips: Timing, Extras and Getting There

Best time to go

Aquaventure opens at 09:15 and closes at sunset. Be at the gate at opening and ride the big slides first, before the headline queues build. LEGOLAND Water Park is closed on Wednesdays, and Yas Waterworld is ladies-only on Fridays.

What costs extra

At Atlantis, towels start at AED 35 and lockers at AED 80, and the AquaXpress fast pass is sold on-site for AED 225–795 depending on the date (entry not included). The price swings with the season, so check it for your date before deciding. Atlantis and LEGOLAND Water Park do not allow outside food or drinks, so budget for lunch inside.

What to bring

Swimwear that meets the rules (no thongs, transparent swimwear, denim or loose clothing such as abayas in the water), your own towel, water shoes, high-SPF sunscreen and an empty bottle. Bring a passport or ID for Dubai Parks and for proof of a child's age at Atlantis.

Kids and height rules

Atlantis prices by height: adult rate from age 7 or above 1.2 m, child rate for ages 3 to 6.99 under 1.2 m, under 2s free with ID. Some rides are restricted to guests over 1.2 m, and under-12s must be with an adult.

Getting there

Aquaventure is at Atlantis on Palm Jumeirah; if you want to sleep next to it, see our best Palm Jumeirah hotels. Dubai Parks and Resorts is on Sheikh Zayed Road about 25 minutes from Dubai Marina, and the LEGOLAND Water Park ticket includes parking. Yas Island is in Abu Dhabi, more than an hour from Dubai by road; none of the Yas tickets include transport from Dubai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best water park in Dubai?

Aquaventure World at Atlantis, The Palm. It has 105+ slides, a private beach and large kids zones for €77 per person, the lowest price per slide of any park on this page. For young children, LEGOLAND Water Park is the calmer pick.

How much are Aquaventure tickets?

From €77 per person for a full-day pass, €81 with The Lost World Aquarium, and €216 with the dolphin encounter. On top, budget AED 35+ for a towel, AED 80+ for a locker and AED 225–795 if you want the AquaXpress fast pass.

Is Yas Waterworld in Dubai?

No. Yas Waterworld is on Yas Island in Abu Dhabi, more than an hour from Dubai by road. Transport is not included in the ticket, so treat it as a day trip or book it if you are staying in Abu Dhabi.

Is LEGOLAND Water Park good for toddlers?

Yes, it is designed for families with young kids and has the DUPLO Splash Safari toddler area. Some rides have height limits (the ticket terms mention 120 cm), and the park is closed on Wednesdays.

Can I bring food or towels into Aquaventure?

Towels yes, and it saves you AED 35 or more. Outside food and drinks are not allowed. Lockers start at AED 80.

Is the dolphin encounter a swim with dolphins?

No. It is a shallow-water interaction: 30 minutes of briefing, then 30 minutes standing in waist-deep water with the dolphins. All ages can join, children 2 and under free, and the same-day waterpark pass is included.
