10 Best Family Resorts and Hotels in Dubai in 2026 (Kids' Clubs, Waterparks and Prices Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Dubai family resorts compared on nightly price, kids' club, waterpark or theme-park access, family rooms and extra-bed rules
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most families the Dubai resort to book is Atlantis, The Palm at €381 a night: daily Aquaventure World and Lost World Aquarium access is included, with a supervised kids' club and 16 restaurants. The value waterpark pick is Le Meridien Mina Seyahi at €235 (unlimited Jungle Bay waterpark, kids' club, 1,200 m beach), Lapita at €235 puts theme-park access in the room rate a short walk from LEGOLAND and MOTIONGATE, and Rove At The Park at €76 is the cheapest bed next to the parks.

The catch most families miss: every price here is for two adults, and four of the ten have no extra beds at all; the rest charge AED 100–200 per extra bed per night. All 10 were compared on price for the same night, kids' club, waterpark or theme-park access, family room size, child and extra-bed rules, and beach. Planning a Family Stay in Dubai: Areas, Getting Around and Extra Costs

Which area suits which family

  • Palm Jumeirah (Atlantis, Hilton): location scores 9.3–9.4, private beaches, and Aquaventure on the Palm itself. Mall of the Emirates is about 20 minutes and Burj Khalifa 25 minutes from Atlantis by car.
  • Jumeirah and Al Sufouh beaches (Jumeirah Beach Hotel, Le Meridien): beach resorts with waterpark access in the room rate (Wild Wadi and Jungle Bay). Jumeirah Beach Hotel scores 9.5 for location.
  • Dubai Parks and Resorts (Lapita, Rove, LEGOLAND Hotel): walk to LEGOLAND, LEGOLAND Water Park, MOTIONGATE and Real Madrid World, but you are out of town (Al Maktoum International is 19 km away). Location scores are 7.9–8.7.
  • City (Grand Hyatt, Al Jaddaf Rotana): no beach, but 15 minutes or a short metro ride from Downtown and Dubai Mall.
  • Dubai Islands (Centara Mirage): beach resort 11 km from Dubai International Airport.

Getting around

Al Jaddaf Rotana is the only pick with a metro station on foot (5 minutes to Al Jaddaf). Lapita includes a shuttle to Mall of the Emirates and Dubai Mall and beach access at Sheraton JBR. Everywhere else, plan on taxis or a car: parking is free at most of the ten.

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: the nightly prices above exclude them. Where the rates break them down, a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and a Dubai tourism fee of AED 10–20 per room per night are added on top.
  • Extra beds: AED 100 (Al Jaddaf Rotana), 125 (Le Meridien), 150 (Lapita, Hilton Palm, Grand Hyatt) or 200 (Jumeirah Beach Hotel) a night. Atlantis, Rove, Centara and LEGOLAND Hotel have none. Cots are free everywhere they are offered.
  • When kids pay as adults: from 12 at Grand Hyatt, from 18 at Le Meridien, Hilton Palm and Al Jaddaf Rotana.
  • Cash deposits: AED 1,000 at Grand Hyatt and AED 500 at Centara, refunded at check-out.
  • Breakfast: €30 (Al Jaddaf Rotana), €33 (Le Meridien), about €29 more at Hilton Palm; included in Centara's half board, LEGOLAND's €248 themed rate and Rove's €213 ticket rate.
  • Waterpark and park tickets: included at Atlantis, Le Meridien and Jumeirah Beach Hotel; on eligible rates at Grand Hyatt; in Lapita's room rate; in LEGOLAND Hotel's themed rate and on Rove's ticket rates. Centara has an on-site waterpark, but its rates do not say whether entry is included. Elsewhere, compare water park tickets before you go.

When to go

The rates here are for mid-January, in the cooler winter months. For an evening away from the resort, compare Dubai dinner cruises.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best family resort in Dubai?

Atlantis, The Palm, from €381 a night for two adults. It includes daily Aquaventure World and Lost World Aquarium access, a supervised kids' club and 16 restaurants, with a 9.2 guest score. For a cheaper waterpark resort, Le Meridien Mina Seyahi (€235) includes unlimited Jungle Bay access and has a kids' club and beach.

Which Dubai hotels include waterpark access?

Atlantis, The Palm (Aquaventure, daily), Jumeirah Beach Hotel (Wild Wadi, daily, plus kids' club access) and Le Meridien Mina Seyahi (unlimited Jungle Bay) include it in the room rate. Grand Hyatt Dubai includes its own waterpark on eligible rate plans, and LEGOLAND Hotel's themed rates include park access.

Which Dubai hotel is best for LEGOLAND and MOTIONGATE?

Lapita (€235) is a short walk from LEGOLAND, LEGOLAND Water Park, MOTIONGATE and Real Madrid World, with theme-park access in the room rate, a kids' club and four-guest rooms from €297 with breakfast. On a budget, Rove At The Park starts at €76; for themed bunk-bed rooms, LEGOLAND Hotel starts at €248 with park access and breakfast. See our theme parks comparison to choose the park.

Can a family of four stay in one room in Dubai?

Yes, at some resorts. Le Meridien Mina Seyahi prices its Deluxe room for four at €359, Lapita sells two-double rooms for four from €297 with breakfast, and Jumeirah Beach Hotel has a Family Deluxe Room with Murphy wall beds. Atlantis, Rove, Centara and LEGOLAND Hotel have no extra beds, so check the room's occupancy before booking.

Do children stay free in Dubai hotels?

Cots for babies (usually 0–2, 0–3 at Atlantis, Hilton Palm and Grand Hyatt) are free wherever a price is listed. Extra beds cost AED 100–200 a night, except at Al Jaddaf Rotana, where they are free for under-3s. Children are charged as adults from 12 at Grand Hyatt and from 18 at Le Meridien, Hilton Palm and Al Jaddaf Rotana.

How much does a family resort in Dubai cost per night?

For two adults on a January weeknight, the ten options here ran from €76 (Rove At The Park) to €471 (Jumeirah Beach Hotel), with beach resorts with a kids' club mostly at €235–381. A service charge (usually 10%), VAT, a municipality fee and a tourism fee of AED 10–20 per room per night come on top, plus extra beds for children.

Which Dubai hotel has the best kids' club on the beach?

For younger children, Hilton Dubai Palm Jumeirah's Pirate's Kids Club (toys, films, sandcastles, shaded kids' pool) at €254 a night, with a 9.0 score. Atlantis has a fully supervised daily programme, Le Meridien adds beach games and two kids' pools, and Jumeirah Beach Hotel includes kids' club access in every room rate.
