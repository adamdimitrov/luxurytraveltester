6 Best Musandam Tours from Dubai in 2026 (Dibba and Khasab Dhow Cruises Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
6 Musandam day trips compared on price, border fees, time on the dhow, pickup and passport rules
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most people the best Musandam day trip from Dubai is the Dibba dhow cruise at €59: about 6 hours on the water with a buffet lunch, snorkelling, a banana boat and a speedboat ride, and you are through the border by 09:30. If dolphins in the fjords are the point, pay for Khasab (from €121) and accept roughly 3.5 hours in a van each way.

Dibba vs Khasab comes down to drive time, budget and your passport. Both trips cross into Oman, so every one needs your original passport, usually valid for 6 months, and on some trips certain nationalities need a UAE residence visa. Border fees of AED 30–85 are extra on some trips and included on others, so the lowest sticker price is not always the cheapest day.

Below are the 6 Musandam trips from Dubai worth booking, 3 from Dibba and 3 from Khasab, with a table of fees, hours on the dhow, price per boat hour and the passport rules each one lists.

Dibba vs Khasab: Which Side of Musandam?

Dibba is the closer port. On the #1 trip you leave at 07:00 and reach the border at 09:30, and the day is a busy water-sports programme: snorkelling, banana boat, speedboat, kayaks, a beach and a cave. Dolphins appear on one itinerary, but they are not the point. Three of the six trips sail from here, from €55 to €87.

Khasab is the farther port and the drive is the price you pay: about 3.5 hours out and 3 back on the #2 trip. In return you get the long, quiet cruise through the Khor Ash Sham (Khor Sham) fjord, dolphin watching built into every Khasab listing, and snorkel stops at Telegraph Island and Seebi Island. Practical Tips: Passports, Border Fees and What to Bring

Passport and visa

Carry your original passport; the €55 Dibba trip states that photocopies are not accepted. The #1, #2 and #4 trips ask for 6 months' validity, the small-group Khasab trip 90+ days and the 365 Adventures Dibba trip 3 months; the #3 listing gives no validity rule. The #2 Khasab trip lists the nationalities that get a visa on arrival (including the UK, US, Canada, Australia, the EU countries, Japan and South Korea); other nationalities need a UAE residence visa valid at least 3 months. The #3 and #4 trips ask UAE residents to bring their Emirates ID as well. The #1 Dibba trip wants a passport copy by WhatsApp at least 24 hours ahead, and the €55 Dibba trip states that there is no refund if Omani immigration refuses entry.

What costs extra

Budget for the UAE exit fee (AED 30.42–35) on the #1, #2 and #6 trips, the Oman visa (AED 50) on #6 and on #2 where your nationality needs one. On #6 the fees apply to children and infants too. Fees are already included on the #3 and #5 Khasab trips, and the #4 listing does not mention them, so ask before you go. Alcohol is not allowed on most of these trips.

What to bring

Passport, swimwear, sunscreen (biodegradable is requested on the Khasab dolphin trip), sunglasses and, on the cheapest Dibba trip, your own towel. Leave large bags at the hotel: the #2 trip does not allow luggage. The #1, #3 and #4 trips are not suitable for pregnant women, and the €55 Dibba cruise warns off anyone prone to seasickness.

Make a week of it

Musandam is a full day, so keep the next one easy. For an evening on the water closer to home, see the best dinner cruises in Dubai or a boat tour from Dubai Marina. If the snorkel stops leave you wanting more, compare scuba diving in Dubai. Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dibba or Khasab better for a Musandam day trip from Dubai?

Dibba for value and a shorter drive: the #1 trip costs €59, reaches the border by 09:30 and gives about 6 hours on the dhow with water sports. Khasab for dolphins and the Khor Ash Sham fjord: from €121, with roughly 3.5 hours in a van each way.

Do I need a visa to visit Musandam from Dubai?

You cross into Oman, so you need your original passport, and each listing sets its own rules. The Khasab dolphin trip (#2) lists the nationalities that get a visa on arrival, including UK, US, Canadian, Australian and EU passports; other nationalities on that trip need a UAE residence visa valid at least 3 months. The #3 and #4 trips ask UAE residents for their Emirates ID too. Check the passport column in the table for your trip.

How much are the border fees for Musandam?

The UAE exit fee is AED 30.42–35 per passport and the Oman visa, where charged, AED 50. The €121 Khasab cruise and the €141 small-group Khasab cruise include border fees. The #1, #2 and #6 trips add them on the day, and the #4 listing does not mention them.

How long is the drive from Dubai to Musandam?

To Dibba, about 2.5 hours to the border (07:00 pickup, 09:30 border). To Khasab, about 3.5 hours out and 3 hours back. Border formalities add time both ways, and a full Khasab day runs from about 06:00 to 19:00.

Will I see dolphins on a Musandam tour?

Dolphin watching is part of every Khasab trip on this page, and several reviewers on the #2 trip mention seeing them in the fjord. On the Dibba side only the #1 trip lists dolphin watching on its itinerary.

Is lunch included on a Musandam dhow cruise?

Yes, on all six trips. Most serve a buffet on board with soft drinks and water; the #1 Dibba trip adds evening tea and snacks, and the small-group Khasab cruise serves dates, Arabic coffee and unlimited soft drinks.

What should I bring on a Musandam trip?

Your original passport (and Emirates ID if you live in the UAE), swimwear, sunscreen, sunglasses and a towel on the €55 Dibba trip. Snorkel gear is provided on every trip. On the #1 trip the exit fee is preferably paid in cash, so carry dirhams unless your trip includes the fees.
