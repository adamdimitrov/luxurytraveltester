10 Best Hotels on Dubai Creek in 2026 (Deira, Al Seef and Creek Harbour Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Creek hotels compared on nightly price, score, room size, pool, breakfast and metro
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most people the best Dubai Creek hotel is Mövenpick Dubai Creek in Deira: a 5-star with a rooftop pool over the water for €86 a night. On a tighter budget, Jannah in Deira scores 9.0 for €66. If you want a beach and an infinity pool, cross to Creek Harbour, where Vida Creek Beach costs €148, and for old Dubai on foot, Canopy by Hilton in Al Seef is €95.

We lined up 10 hotels across all three sides of the Creek (Deira, the Bur Dubai bank from Al Seef to Al Jaddaf, and Creek Harbour) and compared them on the price for the same night, guest and location scores, room size, pool or beach, breakfast cost and metro access. Deira, Bur Dubai or Creek Harbour: the Three Sides of the Creek

"Dubai Creek hotel" covers three very different stays. Pick the side first, then the hotel.

1. Deira (north-east bank): old Dubai, lowest prices, closest to the airport

Deira is the busy trading side of the Creek. This is where the money goes furthest: Mövenpick Dubai Creek (#1) is a 5-star for €86 and Jannah (#7) is €66. Both are 3–5 km from Dubai International Airport, and Mövenpick is about 10 minutes by car from the gold and textile markets. Choose Deira for value, souks and short airport transfers. If you only need a bed near the terminal, our Dubai airport hotels comparison covers that job directly.

2. Bur Dubai side (south-west bank): Al Seef heritage, Wafi and the metro hotels

The opposite bank runs from the restored Al Seef waterfront inland to Oud Metha (Wafi), Dubai Healthcare City and Al Jaddaf. Canopy by Hilton (#5) is the Al Seef pick at €95 with a 9.2 location score. Further inland, Raffles (#3) and Sofitel (#6) sit by Wafi mall with metro access, and the Hyatt residences (#9) and Al Jaddaf Rotana (#8) are both 5 minutes on foot from a metro station. This side suits travellers who want heritage streets or big rooms and the metro, rather than a beach.

3. Dubai Creek Harbour (east bank, further upstream): new towers, pools and the beach

Creek Harbour is the new waterfront district 0.6–0.8 miles from Dubai Festival City. Hotels here are modern and pool-focused: Vida Creek Beach (#2) is beachfront with an infinity pool, Address Creek Harbour (#4) adds a spa and beach access, and Vida Creek Harbour (#10) is the cheapest way in at €108. Prices start at €108 here versus €66–86 for the two Deira picks, and Dubai Mall is about 10 km away.

Practical Tips: Fees, Getting Around and What Costs Extra

What the nightly price does not include

On the rates we compared, a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and the Dubai tourism fee are added to the nightly price. The tourism fee is AED 15 a night at the 4-star hotels here (Jannah, Canopy, Vida Creek Harbour) and AED 20 at the 5-stars (Mövenpick, Raffles, Address, Al Jaddaf Rotana), rising to AED 40–60 on Rotana's larger suites. Two hotels take a refundable deposit on arrival: AED 500 in cash at Mövenpick and AED 200 at the Hyatt residences.

Getting around

For the metro, the Bur Dubai side wins: Al Jaddaf Rotana and the Hyatt residences are each 5 minutes on foot from a station, and Raffles and Sofitel have metro access at Wafi. Deira is the side for the airport (Jannah 3 km, Mövenpick 5 km). Creek Harbour is the most car-dependent; Vida Creek Harbour runs a shuttle, and all three Creek Harbour hotels offer free parking.

Kids, extra beds and arrival times

Extra-bed charges range from AED 90 (Jannah) to AED 400 (Raffles) a night, Vida Creek Harbour has no extra beds at all, and Sofitel charges children as adults from age 5. Check the age rules on the card before you book. The Hyatt residences only check guests in from 15:00 to 19:00, so they are a poor match for late flights.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to stay in Deira or Bur Dubai?

Stay in Deira for the lowest prices and the shortest airport transfer: Mövenpick Dubai Creek is a 5-star for €86 and 5 km from the airport. Stay on the Bur Dubai side for the Al Seef heritage quarter (Canopy by Hilton, €95) or for metro access and bigger rooms around Wafi and Al Jaddaf.

What is the best hotel on Dubai Creek?

For most travellers, Mövenpick Dubai Creek: a 5-star on the Deira bank with a rooftop pool overlooking the Creek, a 9.2 guest score and a price of €86 a night. The highest-rated hotel in the comparison is Vida Creek Beach in Creek Harbour (9.3), at €148.

Are there Dubai Creek hotels with a beach?

Yes, in Dubai Creek Harbour. Vida Creek Beach Hotel is listed as beachfront and has an infinity pool and kids' pool; Address Creek Harbour lists beach access with its infinity pool. The Deira and Bur Dubai hotels have pools only.

Which Creek hotels are closest to the airport?

The Deira hotels. Jannah Dubai Creek Hotel is 3 km from Dubai International Airport and Mövenpick Dubai Creek 5 km, with an airport shuttle on request. The Hyatt Regency Creek Heights Residences in Healthcare City is 4 km away. For more options next to the terminal, see our Dubai airport hotels comparison.

Which Dubai Creek hotel is best for families?

For space, Al Jaddaf Rotana: an 80 m² one-bedroom suite with two bathrooms and a kitchen for €167, free cots and extra beds under 3, and only guests 18+ charged as adults. For pools and a beach, Vida Creek Beach or Address Creek Harbour, both with kids' pools and free cots up to age 3.

How much is the Dubai tourism fee at Creek hotels?

On the rates compared here it is AED 15 per night at the 4-star hotels and AED 20 at the 5-stars, up to AED 40–60 for larger suites at Al Jaddaf Rotana. It comes on top of the room price together with 5% VAT and a 7% municipality fee.

Is Dubai Creek Harbour a good place to stay?

It is the best side of the Creek for pools and a beach and the newest hotels, with location scores of 9.2–9.4 for the three hotels here. It costs more than Deira (from €108 at Vida Creek Harbour versus €66–86 in Deira) and is about 10 km from Dubai Mall, so it suits a relaxed stay more than non-stop sightseeing.
