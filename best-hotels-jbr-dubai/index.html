10 Best Hotels in JBR (Jumeirah Beach Residence) in 2026 (Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 JBR hotels compared on nightly price, beach access, breakfast, room size and location score
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most people the right JBR hotel is the Sofitel Dubai Jumeirah Beach at €174 a night: it sits on The Walk with the beach in front, scores 9.2, and extra beds and cots for kids are free. On a tighter budget, Mövenpick Hotel Jumeirah Beach gets you a 5-star room for €136 a short walk back from the sand, and Ramada JBR is the cheapest solid stay at €116. If price is no object, The Ritz-Carlton's private beach and six pools start at €620.

All 10 were compared for the same night on price, guest score, location score, beach and pool, what breakfast costs and the size of the cheapest room. Use the filters for beachfront, suites and apartments, families, couples or €150 and under, or scroll the ranking below.

Where to Stay in JBR, Getting Around and What Costs Extra

Which part of JBR

The Walk, JBR's 1.7 km promenade, runs along the beach at the foot of the towers. Hotels split three ways: beachfront on The Walk (Sofitel, Hilton Dubai Jumeirah, Amwaj Rotana, DoubleTree, FIVE LUXE), resorts at the ends (The Ritz-Carlton's gardens and six pools, and Le Royal Meridien on Al Mamsha Street, a short walk from JBR), and hotels a walk from the sand (Mövenpick, Ramada and Suha), which cost less.

Getting around

The Dubai Tram runs along JBR: the JBR 2 stop is a 3-minute walk from Amwaj Rotana, 5 minutes from DoubleTree and 500 m from the Sofitel. Hilton Dubai Jumeirah is a short walk from the metro station by Dubai Marina Mall, and Jumeirah Lakes Towers metro is close to Amwaj Rotana. Mall of the Emirates is 10–15 minutes by car from most of these hotels, and Dubai International Airport about 30 minutes. Every hotel here has free parking.

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: rates exclude 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and the Dubai tourism fee of AED 15–20 per room per night (AED 15 at Ramada and the DoubleTree one-bedroom suite, AED 20 at Mövenpick, Hilton, Le Royal Meridien and the Ritz-Carlton). A 10% service charge is added on top as well.
  • Breakfast: €20 at Mövenpick, €25 at Ramada; included at Hilton Dubai Jumeirah and Suha on the rates shown.
  • Extra beds: free at the Sofitel and (for 12+) the Ritz-Carlton; AED 150 at DoubleTree, AED 200 at Hilton and FIVE LUXE, AED 216 at Amwaj Rotana, AED 360 at Le Royal Meridien. Ramada charges AED 150; Mövenpick has no extra beds.
  • Deposits and age rules: FIVE LUXE takes a AED 1,000 damage deposit by card; Hilton Dubai Jumeirah requires the lead guest to be 21.

Hilton Dubai Jumeirah and Le Royal Meridien offer water sports from their beaches, and Amwaj Rotana has the water sports at The Beach on its doorstep. Frequently Asked Questions

Which JBR hotels are directly on the beach?

Sofitel Dubai Jumeirah Beach, Hilton Dubai Jumeirah, FIVE LUXE, Amwaj Rotana (about 100 steps to the sand), DoubleTree (private beach access) and The Ritz-Carlton (private beach) are on the beach. Le Royal Meridien is beachfront a short walk from JBR. Mövenpick, Ramada and Suha are a short walk back from it.

What is the cheapest good hotel in JBR?

Ramada by Wyndham Dubai JBR at €116 a night for a 484 ft² room, scored 8.8. The cheapest 5-star is Mövenpick Hotel Jumeirah Beach at €136, and the cheapest stay with breakfast included is Suha JBR at €143 for a one-bedroom apartment (a members' price after a free sign-in; without it, Mövenpick's €150 breakfast rate is cheaper).

Is it better to stay in JBR or Dubai Marina?

Stay in JBR if the beach is the point of the trip: several hotels open straight onto it. Dubai Marina puts you on the yacht-lined canal with the beach a walk away. The two areas sit side by side, so you can easily use both; compare options in our Dubai Marina hotels guide.

Which JBR hotel is best for families?

Hilton Dubai Jumeirah (€217 with breakfast) has a kids' club, a beachside playground and a family pool, but the lead guest must be 21. The Ritz-Carlton has Ritz Kids and six pools at €620. For space, the DoubleTree one-bedroom suite (835 ft², €232) has a sofa bed, and the Sofitel gives free extra beds. More options are in our best family resorts in Dubai.

Do JBR hotel prices include breakfast?

Usually not. Of these 10, only Hilton Dubai Jumeirah (€217) and Suha JBR (€143, member price) include breakfast in the lowest rate. Mövenpick charges €20 and Ramada €25; the Ritz-Carlton, Le Royal Meridien and DoubleTree sell breakfast-included rates at a higher price.

What taxes and fees are added to a JBR hotel bill?

Expect a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and the Dubai tourism fee of AED 15–20 per night on top of the room rate. FIVE LUXE also takes a refundable AED 1,000 damage deposit on arrival.

Is JBR a good base for activities?

Yes for the water: several of these hotels run water sports straight from their beach, and Dubai Marina is next door. For more, see our scuba diving and adrenaline experiences comparisons. The tram and metro connect you to the rest of the city.
