7 Best Dubai Attraction Passes in 2026 (and When a Pass Is Worth It)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
7 Dubai attraction passes compared on price, picks, validity and savings vs single tickets
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

A Dubai attraction pass saves money only if you spend it on the expensive tickets. The 3-pick Dubai Top Attractions Pass (€179) beats buying separately when you choose Burj Khalifa, Aquaventure and IMG Worlds: those three cost €209.27 on their own, so you keep €30.56. Swap IMG for the €13 Dubai Frame and the same pass costs you €19.71 more than single tickets.

Not chasing the big parks? The 5-day hop-on hop-off pass with Lost World Aquarium and Dubai Frame (€63) is the best-rated deal here at 4.4 out of 5, and it costs less than a 1-day bus-and-aquarium ticket plus a Frame entry bought separately.

What decides it: how many paid attractions you will really fit into the validity window (7 days on most passes, 30 on Go City), whether your must-sees are on the list (the €409 Unlimited pass has no IMG Worlds), and the ratings, which are low for this category: four of the seven passes score under 4 out of 5. Below, each pass has its math worked out against single tickets.

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

This is the pass most people mean when they search for the Dubai Pass: one price, then 3, 5 or 7 picks from a list of 38 attractions over 7 days. The list is the strongest on the page because it holds all three of the city's priciest single tickets: At the Top Burj Khalifa, Atlantis Aquaventure and IMG Worlds of Adventure, plus Wild Wadi, Legoland, Motiongate, Ski Dubai, iFly and La Perle.

The math works only when you aim high. Bought separately, a Level 124/125 Burj Khalifa ticket (€69.01), an Aquaventure day pass (€76.96) and IMG Worlds (€63.30) total €209.27. The 3-pick pass is €178.71, so you keep €30.56. Spend one of the three picks on the Dubai Frame (€13.03) and the separate total drops to €159.00, which makes the pass €19.71 dearer. One guest put it plainly: check the attraction prices and pick the most expensive ones.

The catches are practical. You need the Smartvisit app to reserve time slots, some venues still swap the pass for a paper ticket at the counter, and attractions can drop off the list (a 2023 review of the sister Select pass mentions Wild Wadi disappearing before a trip). The 3.9 out of 5 rating reflects that friction rather than the price. For the headliners, it is still the best-value flexible pass here.

Is a Dubai Pass Worth It? The Math

The 3-step test

1. Write down the paid attractions you would book anyway. 2. Add up their single prices. 3. Compare with the pass for the same number of picks. If the pass is not at least 10% cheaper, buy singles: they have no time windows, no pools and no app to manage.

Worked example: the big three

Burj Khalifa Level 124/125 (€69.01) + Atlantis Aquaventure (€76.96) + IMG Worlds (€63.30) = €209.27. On the Top Attractions Pass that trio costs €178.71, a saving of €30.56 (about 15%). The same three are not possible on the Select pass (Burj Khalifa and Aquaventure share Pool 1, and IMG is not listed), the Unlimited pass (no IMG) or the SuperSaver (no Aquaventure).

Worked example: the budget trio

Burj Khalifa (€69.01) + IMG Worlds (€63.30) + Dubai Frame (€13.03) = €145.34. The two passes that can hold this trio cost more for 3 picks: €156.83 (SuperSaver) and €178.71 (Top Attractions). With cheap attractions in the mix, singles win.

Worked example: bus days

The 1-day City Sightseeing bus with Lost World Aquarium is €51.06; add the Dubai Frame (€13.03) and you are at €64.09. The 5-day Big Bus pass with both attractions is €62.78. If you will use the bus on more than one day, the 5-day pass is the obvious buy.

Practical Tips: Booking Slots, Validity and Extras

Booking time slots

None of the pick-your-own passes is a walk-in ticket for everything. The Dubai Pass products need the Smartvisit app to reserve dates and time slots, Go City uses its own app, and the SuperSaver is booked through the provider's team or WhatsApp. Reserve Burj Khalifa and the parks first: they are the picks that make the pass pay.

When the clock starts

Validity runs from your first attraction, not from purchase: 7 consecutive days on the Top Attractions and Select passes, 3 or 5 on Unlimited, 6 to 8 on the SuperSaver, 30 on Go City. Plan the first redemption for the day your busy stretch begins.

What costs extra

None of the passes includes transport between attractions, except the tours on the SuperSaver that list transfers, and the two bus passes, where the bus is the transport. Meals, tips and optional extras are not included. Hotel pickup is not part of either bus pass.

Check the list before you pay

Every pass warns that attractions can change. Seasonal items such as Miracle Garden and Global Village close for part of the year, and guests on the Smartvisit passes report attractions leaving the list. Confirm your must-sees in the app the week you travel. Comparing a safari on its own? See our desert safaris in Dubai.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Dubai Pass worth it?

Only if your picks are big-ticket attractions. Burj Khalifa (€69.01), Aquaventure (€76.96) and IMG Worlds (€63.30) cost €209.27 separately versus €178.71 on the 3-pick Top Attractions Pass, a €30.56 saving. Swap in cheaper picks like the Dubai Frame (€13.03) and single tickets come out cheaper.

Which Dubai attraction pass is best?

For Burj Khalifa, Aquaventure and IMG Worlds, the Dubai Top Attractions Pass (from €179, 7 days). For relaxed sightseeing, the 5-day Big Bus pass with Lost World Aquarium and Dubai Frame (€63), the best-rated option at 4.4 out of 5.

Go City Dubai vs Dubai Pass: which is better?

The Dubai Pass (Top Attractions, Select, Unlimited) includes Burj Khalifa At the Top and Aquaventure and has a longer review record (37 to 164 reviews per pass). The Go City Highlights Pass gives 30 days and adds Abu Dhabi and inside Burj Al Arab, but has only 3 reviews averaging 2.0 out of 5 and does not name Burj Khalifa or Aquaventure on its list.

Does a Dubai pass include Burj Khalifa?

The Top Attractions, Select, Unlimited and SuperSaver passes include Burj Khalifa (At the Top, or Level 124 on the SuperSaver). The two bus passes do not; pair them with a ticket from our Burj Khalifa tickets comparison.

Is the Dubai Unlimited Pass worth it?

Rarely. At €409.39 you need around €410 of tickets in 3 days. Burj Khalifa, Aquaventure, the bus with Lost World Aquarium and the Dubai Frame together are only €210.06, and there is a 1-hour gap rule between attractions. It is rated 3.2 out of 5 and does not include IMG Worlds.

How long is a Dubai attraction pass valid?

From the first attraction: 7 days for the Top Attractions and Select passes, 3 or 5 days for Unlimited, 6 to 8 days for the SuperSaver (by number of picks), 5 days for the Big Bus pass and 30 days for Go City. The City Sightseeing bus ticket covers 1, 2 or 3 days and can be used any day within 12 months.

Do Dubai passes include transport?

No pick-your-own pass includes transport between attractions, apart from the SuperSaver tours that list transfers (dhow cruises, desert safari). The two hop-on hop-off passes are the transport, covering Downtown, the Creek souks, Madinat Jumeirah, Atlantis and the Marina.
