Abu Dhabi Tourism Statistics 2026 (Visitors, Hotels & Attractions)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
Compiled from official DCT Abu Dhabi data • 7 Charts with Data Tables • Methodology
Last updated: Nov 19, 2026

Abu Dhabi recorded 26.6 million visitors in 2025 and its hotels welcomed 5.9 million guests, filling 81% of rooms. Hotel revenue grew far faster than guest numbers: AED 9.1 billion, up 19.5%, because the average room rate rose 19%.

This report covers how many people visit the UAE capital, which countries they come from, how long they stay, how the three regions compare and which attractions draw the crowds, all from the Department of Culture and Tourism (DCT). For the neighbouring emirate, see our Dubai tourism statistics; for the national picture, UAE tourism statistics.

How many tourists visit Abu Dhabi each year?

Abu Dhabi recorded 26.6 million visitors in 2025, up from 24 million in 2023, about 11% more in two years. The emirate's target is 39.3 million visitors a year by 2030 under the Abu Dhabi Tourism Strategy 2030.

The visitor total is a broad measure: it includes day visitors from Dubai and the other emirates, who make up a large share because the two cities are 90 minutes apart by road. The tighter measure is hotel guests, which grew from 5.50 million in 2023 to 5.82 million in 2024 and 5.9 million in 2025.

Guest numbers grew only 2.2% in 2025, but the guest mix shifted abroad: international guests rose 10% while domestic stays (UAE residents on staycations) fell back. International guests spend more per night, which is why revenue grew nine times faster than guest numbers. In 2024, 3 million of the 5.8 million hotel guests came from abroad.

Where do Abu Dhabi's tourists come from?

India sent 436,124 hotel guests to Abu Dhabi in 2025, 22% more than in 2024 and 13% of all hotel guests. Russia (257,200), the United Kingdom (250,906), China (248,494) and Saudi Arabia (200,652) complete the top five.

India is far ahead: it sends 70% more hotel guests than the next market. Behind it, Russia, the UK and China are almost level at about 250,000 guests each. Chinese guests also stayed 13% longer than a year earlier, the fastest increase of any key market.

How long do visitors stay in Abu Dhabi?

Guests stayed 2.9 nights on average across all accommodation in 2025, 3% longer than in 2024 and almost a night shorter than in Dubai (3.7 nights). Long-haul European markets stay the longest.

How are Abu Dhabi's hotels performing?

Hotel revenue reached AED 9.1 billion in 2025, up 19.5%. Occupancy averaged 81%, the average daily rate rose 19% and revenue per available room 23%, while airlines added 11% more inbound seats and flew them 89% full.

The story of 2025 is price, not volume: guest numbers grew 2.2%, but each room earned almost a quarter more. At the end of 2024 the emirate had 171 hotel establishments with more than 34,000 rooms, about a fifth of Dubai's room count, which keeps occupancy high when big events (Formula 1, concerts, exhibitions) fill the city.

Which parts of Abu Dhabi get the most visitors?

The emirate has three regions. The Abu Dhabi region (the city, Yas and Saadiyat islands) takes about nine in ten hotel guests. Al Ain, the garden city on the Omani border, had 473,100 guests (+9%), and Al Dhafra, the desert region with Liwa and the Empty Quarter, 147,900 (+3%).

Al Dhafra is small but growing in value: its hotel occupancy rose 19% in 2025, helped by desert events such as the Liwa festival village, which drew more than 159,000 visitors.

What are the most visited attractions in Abu Dhabi?

The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque is by far the most visited landmark: 6,846,723 guests in 2025, its highest total on record and 4% more than in 2024. That figure includes 4,331,046 visitors, 1,531,192 worshippers and 898,767 iftar guests during Ramadan. Cultural sites and libraries across the emirate recorded more than 8.6 million visits.

Frequently asked questions

How many tourists visited Abu Dhabi in 2025? 26.6 million visitors, including 5.9 million hotel guests, according to the Department of Culture and Tourism Abu Dhabi.

Which country sends the most tourists to Abu Dhabi? India, with 436,124 hotel guests in 2025 (13% of the total), followed by Russia, the UK, China and Saudi Arabia.

What is the most visited attraction in Abu Dhabi? The Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, with 6.85 million guests in 2025 (4.33 million of them tourist visitors).

How full are Abu Dhabi's hotels? 81% average occupancy in 2025, up from 79% in 2024.

How many visitors does Abu Dhabi want to attract? 39.3 million a year by 2030.
