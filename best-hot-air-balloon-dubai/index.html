Pickup Time, Season, What to Bring and What Costs Extra

Pickup is in the middle of the night. Work back from the itineraries: about 45–50 minutes of driving to the launch site, then 50–60 minutes of coffee, briefing and inflation before take-off at sunrise. Expect your pickup roughly two hours before sunrise; the operator confirms the exact time. Go to bed early and skip the late night out.

Weather sets the flight length. Every operator ties the air time to the conditions on the day (40–70 minutes is the usual range), so treat the longer end as a bonus, not a promise. All seven offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before, which leaves room to move your date if the forecast looks wrong.

Who cannot fly: children under 5 (under 7 on the luxury flight), pregnant women and people with back problems on every flight. Four flights also exclude guests over 120 kg, and four exclude guests over 80.

What to bring: passport or ID (a copy is usually accepted), comfortable shoes, sunglasses, a hat and a warm layer for the pre-dawn wait. Backpacks, large bags, drones and baby strollers are not allowed on most flights. For the luxury flight you submit passport or Emirates ID details for every guest in advance, as UAE rules require.

What costs extra: the full flight video (450 AED on #1, optional on #4 and #6), tips, and pickup outside the standard zones. Jebel Ali and Bab Al Shams are excluded on #1 and #2, and private-only on #7.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a hot air balloon ride in Dubai?

Sunrise flights with hotel pickup cost from €234 to €419 per person. Most sit between €246 and €284. Flight-only starts at €234 (Noble Life) or €249 for the better-reviewed tour; with breakfast and a camel ride from €246, or €271 for the top-rated combo. The luxury flight with a Michelin-chef breakfast is €419.

How long is a hot air balloon flight in Dubai?

Usually 40–70 minutes in the air, depending on the weather. One package lists 60–90 minutes and the luxury flight about 1 hour. With pickup, briefing and the drive back, the whole trip takes 4–5 hours.

What time is pickup for a Dubai balloon ride?

Very early. With a 45–50 minute drive and about an hour of coffee and briefing before a sunrise take-off, pickup is roughly two hours before sunrise. The operator confirms the exact time before your flight.

Is breakfast included?

Only on some. #1 and #7 include a Bedouin camp buffet, #3 a Michelin-chef breakfast, and #5 in its deluxe and premium tiers. The flight-only options serve tea or coffee before take-off.

Can children go on a hot air balloon in Dubai?

Yes, from age 5 on most flights and from 7 on the luxury flight. Baby strollers are not allowed on most of them.

Is there a weight limit?

Four of the seven flights do not take guests over 120 kg (#1, #2, #5 and #7). If this applies to you, pick #3, #4 or #6 and confirm with the operator before booking.

Balloon ride or desert safari?

Do the balloon for a calm sunrise view from 4,000 ft. Do an evening safari for dune bashing and dinner in the desert. They work well on different days; compare options in our desert safari comparison.
