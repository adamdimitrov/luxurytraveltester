10 Best Hotels in Dubai With a Private Pool in 2026 (Prices Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Dubai private-pool villas and suites compared on nightly price, guest score, pool type, size, beach and breakfast
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most couples the private-pool stay to book in Dubai is Meliá Desert Palm at €481 a night (member price): a 198 m² villa with its own pool on a polo estate, 15 minutes from the airport. For a pool near the beach and nightlife, FIVE LUXE at JBR (€440) has the best guest score here; for a villa on the sand, Nikki Beach (€1,036, breakfast included); and the cheapest hotel suite with its own pool is Al Bandar Rotana on Dubai Creek at €429.

What most people miss: a private pool costs 2 to 5 times the same hotel's cheapest room, and the most space for your money is away from the beach. All 10 were compared on the price of the cheapest pool room or villa for the same night, guest and location score, pool type, unit size, beach and breakfast. Booking a Private Pool in Dubai: Pool Types, Areas and Extra Costs

Which kind of private pool

  • Villa pools (Meliá, Nikki Beach, Jumeirah Beach Hotel): a real pool outside a standalone villa, the most private option. Zabeel Saray's four-bedroom villa is listed as 'Pool access', so confirm a private pool with the resort.
  • Suite and apartment pools (One&Only, Al Bandar Rotana, Al Bandar Arjaan, and FIVE LUXE, whose listing names a pool but not a private one): a pool attached to a suite or apartment inside the hotel.
  • Plunge pools (Anantara Downtown): a small pool on a high-floor balcony, for cooling off with a view rather than swimming.
  • Desert dome pool (Al Marmoom): a pool on the deck of a dome lodge in the desert reserve.

Where to stay

  • Palm Jumeirah and Pearl Jumeira (One&Only, Zabeel Saray, Nikki Beach): private beaches and the priciest villas. Nikki Beach is the closest to the city, 15 minutes from Dubai International.
  • Umm Suqeim (Jumeirah Beach Hotel): beachfront, with Wild Wadi included on the villa rate.
  • JBR and Dubai Marina (FIVE LUXE): the best location score, restaurants on foot, Marina Beach 1.2 km.
  • Downtown and Business Bay (Anantara): minutes from Dubai Mall; Dubai International 18 km.
  • Dubai Creek (both Al Bandar properties): the cheapest pools, minutes from Dubai International.
  • Out of town (Meliá on Al Awir Road, Al Marmoom in the desert): space and quiet; plan on taxis or a car.

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: the prices above exclude taxes and charges. Where the rates break them down, a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and a Dubai tourism fee of AED 20 per room per night are added.
  • Breakfast: included at One&Only, Nikki Beach, Jumeirah Beach Hotel (plus dinner), Al Marmoom, Al Bandar Arjaan and Zabeel Saray; €32 at Al Bandar Rotana.
  • Deposits: AED 1,000 at Meliá and FIVE LUXE, AED 500 at Al Bandar Rotana, taken by card on arrival and refunded after check-out.
  • Extra beds: from free (ages 4–11 at Al Marmoom, 4–5 at Nikki Beach) to AED 250 a night at One&Only and Zabeel Saray; none at Meliá.
