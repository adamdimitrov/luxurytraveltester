Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Luxury Travel Tester ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, usage, and protection when you access our website (https://luxurytraveltester.com) and associated content.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal personal data necessary to provide verified travel reviews and editorial content:

  • Usage & Analytics Data: IP addresses (anonymized), browser types, operating systems, referring URLs, and page navigation patterns collected via Google Analytics 4 (GA4).
  • Direct Communications: Information provided when contacting us directly via email (name, email address, message contents).

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information exclusively to:

  • Maintain, operate, and optimize site speed and performance.
  • Understand user reading behavior to improve editorial recommendations.
  • Prevent abuse, security breaches, and fraudulent activity.

3. Cookies and Affiliate Disclosure

Luxury Travel Tester participates in affiliate partner programs (including GetYourGuide and Booking.com). When you click outbound booking links, affiliate cookies may be placed on your device by the respective provider to credit verified reservations at no additional cost to you.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Under GDPR and CCPA regulations, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. We do not sell or monetize personal information to third parties.

5. Contact Information

For inquiries regarding this policy, contact our data protection officer at contact@luxurytraveltester.com.
