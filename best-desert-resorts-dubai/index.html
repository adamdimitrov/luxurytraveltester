8 Best Desert Resorts in and Near Dubai in 2026 (Prices Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
8 desert resorts in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Ras Al Khaimah compared on nightly price, drive time, private pools, breakfast and room size
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most people staying in Dubai, the desert resort to book is Bab Al Shams at €443 a night: 45 minutes from the city, a 9.3 guest score, three pools and four restaurants. For your own pool, the Ritz-Carlton Al Wadi gives you a 158 m² villa with breakfast for €620; the splurge is Al Maha from €992, and the cheapest is Telal in Al Ain at €260.

The catch most visitors miss: only three of these eight are in Dubai itself; the rest are in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Sharjah or Ras Al Khaimah, up to a 2-hour drive beyond Abu Dhabi. All eight were compared on price for the same night, drive time, guest and location score, private pool, breakfast and room size. 📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

Bab Al Shams is the desert resort we would book for most people staying in Dubai. For €443 a night you get a 5★ resort designed like a walled Arab village, set inside the Al Marmoom Reserve on Al Qudra Road, about 45 minutes from the city centre. That is close enough for a one-night escape, and the guest score (9.3 from 1,768 reviews) is backed by more stays than any other resort here.

It is also the most complete resort at its price: three pools (one infinity, one shallow kids' pool), a spa with sauna and steam room, and four restaurants, including Al Hadheerah for a Middle Eastern grill dinner. Location (9.5), cleanliness (9.7) and facilities (9.6) all score high.

Against Al Maha (#3), you give up a private pool and full-board package but pay less than half. Against the Ritz-Carlton Al Wadi (#2), you get a much shorter drive from Dubai but a far smaller room: 37 m² versus a 158 m² villa.

The catches: the €443 room faces the palm courtyard (the Desert View room is €464), breakfast adds about €78 (€521 with free cancellation), and on top come a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and an AED 20 tourism fee per night. Bring cash for the AED 500 deposit.

  • Pools: Infinity pool, outdoor pool and kids' pool with shallow end
  • Getting there: Al Qudra Road, opposite Endurance City, in the Al Marmoom Reserve; about 45 minutes from Dubai's centre
  • Parking: Free private parking
  • Dining: Al Hadheerah, Anwa, Zala and Ninive
  • Spa: Spa and wellness centre, sauna, steam room, massages
  • Policies: Minimum check-in age 18; free cots for 0–3; no extra beds
  • Pets: Not allowed

📝 Review & Quick Facts Overview ▾

Al Maha is the most exclusive desert stay on this page. There are only 42 Bedouin-style villas, each with a temperature-controlled private pool, a sun deck and a soaking tub, set in a reserve where gazelles and other wildlife roam freely. You reach it on an 8 km sand road from the gate, about 45 minutes from Dubai.

The money buys a package rather than a room. Guests can join two activities (falconry at dawn, a dune drive or a nature walk), and full-board rates cover breakfast, a 3-course lunch and a 5-course dinner at Al Diwaan, whose deck looks over the desert. Planning a Desert Stay: Location, Extra Costs and Activities

Dubai desert or a day's drive?

  • Inside Dubai (Bab Al Shams, Al Maha, Al Marmoom Dome): Bab Al Shams and Al Maha are about 45 minutes from the city, and Al Marmoom Dome is 40 km from the centre. These work for a single night.
  • Abu Dhabi emirate (Al Wathba, Qasr al Sarab, Telal in Al Ain): Al Wathba is 48 km from Zayed International Airport; Telal is about an hour from Abu Dhabi's airport; Qasr al Sarab is a 2-hour drive from Abu Dhabi, deep in the Liwa Desert. Plan two nights for Qasr al Sarab.
  • Northern emirates (Ritz-Carlton Al Wadi in Ras Al Khaimah, Moon Retreat in Sharjah, 64 km from Sharjah's airport).

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: the nightly prices above exclude them. Dubai resorts add 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and an AED 20 tourism fee per night; the Abu Dhabi resorts add 5% VAT plus 4% municipality and 4% tourism fees (Al Wathba also a 4% destination tax); Moon Retreat adds 5% VAT and a 10% municipality fee; the Ritz-Carlton adds 5% VAT, AED 20 a night and a 7% destination charge. A 10% service charge (10.5% at the Ritz-Carlton) is also added on top of the nightly prices shown.
  • Breakfast: included at Ritz-Carlton, Qasr al Sarab, Al Marmoom Dome and Moon Retreat; about €78 extra at Bab Al Shams; at Al Wathba the two-adult board option is half board at €475. Al Maha's full board is €1,673 for two.
  • Extra beds: free for 4–11 at Al Marmoom Dome; AED 150–420 a night elsewhere; not available at Bab Al Shams or Moon Retreat.
  • Deposits: Bab Al Shams takes an AED 500 cash deposit on arrival.

Activities and when to go

Al Maha lets guests join two activities (falconry, dune drive or nature walk); the other resorts list activities without saying they are included in the rate. For riding in the dunes see our Dubai horse riding comparison, and for a dawn view over the desert the best hot air balloon flights, and for faster thrills dune buggy tours.
