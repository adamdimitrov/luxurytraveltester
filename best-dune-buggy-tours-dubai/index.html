7 Best Dune Buggy Tours in Dubai in 2026 (Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
7 dune buggy tours compared on driving minutes, buggy power, price per buggy vs per person and pickup
Last updated: Nov 16, 2026

Most "dune buggy" safaris in Dubai give you 30 minutes behind the wheel, and some give you none until you pay for the buggy add-on. If driving is the point, book the Red Dune Desert tour: a full 60 minutes in a twin-seat buggy from €139, then sandboarding, with a camel ride and BBQ camp available as options and pickup from Dubai, Sharjah or Ajman.

On a budget, Excursion Point's €42 safari includes a 30-minute self-drive in a 4-seat buggy plus 4x4 dune bashing, the cheapest buggy minute on this page at €1.41. For a buggy and guide all to yourselves, the Kings and Queens private tour is €222 per group for a 2-seat Polaris RZR 1000cc for an hour, private car included.

What decides it: how many minutes you actually drive, whether the price is per person or per buggy, and how many people share one steering wheel. All three are in the comparison table below the cards. If you want the classic sunset safari with only a short taster drive, our 10 desert safaris compared is the better page.

Per Person vs Per Buggy: How Not to Overpay

Dubai buggy prices come in three flavours, and mixing them up is the most common way to pick wrong:

  • Per group: the Kings and Queens private tour charges €222 per group for one 2-seat buggy. Two people pay about €111 each, which is cheaper per head than the shared #1 tour, but a solo traveller pays the full €222.
  • Per person, buggy included: Red Dune Desert (60 min), Excursion Point (30 min) and the 1000cc Lehbab hour (60 min) include the drive in the from-price. On Excursion Point one buggy carries a driver and up to 3 passengers.
  • Per person, buggy optional: the Red Dunes safari at €27 and 360 Adventures at €24 are entry prices. The buggy is a separate option, priced in the booking calendar. Budget for it before comparing them to the others.

Rule of thumb: if the price looks too good for an hour in a buggy, the buggy is not in it.

Practical Tips: Age Limits, What to Bring and What Costs Extra

Age limits

They vary more than you would expect: Kings and Queens takes children from 4, the Lehbab 1000cc tour from 10, the Sharjah session from 14 with an adult (drivers 16+), and Red Dune Desert and 360 Adventures from 16. Pregnant women and people with back or heart problems are excluded on most tours.

What to bring

Closed comfortable shoes or sports shoes, sunglasses, a sun hat and sunscreen. Leave jewellery, watches and large bags at the hotel: luggage is not allowed on two of the tours, and anything dropped in the sand is hard to find. Helmets are listed on the Kings and Queens, Lehbab 1000cc, Sharjah and 360 Adventures tours; Kings and Queens also gives you goggles.

Getting there

Five of the seven include pickup. The Sharjah session has no pickup, and 360 Adventures sells the transfer as an option, with no Uber or public transport back from the site. Expect around 45 minutes each way from Dubai to the Lahbab and Lehbab dunes.

What costs extra

Alcohol and souvenir photos on Red Dune Desert, food at the Kings and Queens base camp, the quad bike on Excursion Point, and the buggy itself on the Red Dunes safari and 360 Adventures. A falcon photographer approaching you in the desert on the Kings and Queens tour is not part of the tour and sets his own price.

Insurance and waivers

You sign a waiver on the Sharjah session and at 360 Adventures. On the Lehbab 1000cc tour and the Sharjah session, accidents and damage are your own responsibility, so make sure your travel insurance covers motorsport activities.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a dune buggy ride in Dubai?

A 30-minute self-drive buggy session comes bundled with a desert safari from about €42 per person (Excursion Point). A full hour costs from €98 per person in a 1000cc buggy, or €139 on the Red Dune Desert tour, where a BBQ camp evening can be added. A fully private 1000cc buggy for two is €222 per group, about €111 each.

Is the price per person or per buggy?

Most shared tours price per person. The Kings and Queens private tour prices per group (one 2-seat buggy). The €24–27 offers price the safari or a quad as the entry option, with the buggy as a separate option. Always check which option you are selecting.

Do I drive the buggy myself?

Yes, every buggy option on this page is self-drive. On Red Dune Desert and the private tour a guide leads the route and sets the pace. On the private tour you swap seats with your partner after 30 minutes. On Excursion Point's 4-seat buggies one person drives and up to three ride as passengers.

How long do you actually drive?

30 minutes on Excursion Point (the 360 Adventures itinerary lists a 30-minute quad ride), 60 minutes on Red Dune Desert and the Lehbab 1000cc tour, and 60 minutes shared between two on the private Kings and Queens tour. Total trip length is much longer, from 1–2 hours up to 7 hours with a camp dinner.

Can children ride a dune buggy in Dubai?

On the Kings and Queens private tour children from 4 can join, and the Lehbab 1000cc tour takes children from 10. Red Dune Desert and 360 Adventures are 16+. On the Sharjah session the minimum age is 14 with an adult, and drivers must be 16+.

Is a dune buggy tour better than a desert safari?

If you want to drive, yes: a standard safari has someone else driving a 4x4 and maybe a short quad taster. If you want the sunset camp, shows and BBQ with minimal effort, a classic safari is the better buy. Compare both on our desert safari comparison.

Can I cancel?

All seven tours offer free cancellation up to 24 hours before for a full refund, and all let you reserve now and pay later.
