10 Best Hotels Near Dubai Airport (DXB) in 2026 (Prices Compared Side by Side)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 hotels near Dubai International compared on distance to the terminals, shuttle, check-out time, price and guest score
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most layovers and night flights, book Premier Inn Dubai International Airport at €52 a night: it faces Terminal 3, runs a free shuttle every 30 minutes and lets you check out from 01:00. For a five-star on the airport doorstep, Le Méridien Dubai is €161 with five pools; Holiday Inn Express (€66) includes breakfast and check-out until 14:00, and families do best at Holiday Inn & Suites Festival City (€149), where kids eat free.

The catch: only three of the ten are within a kilometre of the terminals, and only four list an airport shuttle. The rest sit 2–3 km away in Garhoud, Port Saeed and Festival City, a short taxi ride. All 10 were compared on distance to DXB, shuttle, check-in and check-out times, price for the same night, guest score, room size and breakfast. Staying Near DXB: Which Area, Getting to the Terminal and Extra Costs

Which side of the airport

  • Garhoud, the terminal side (Premier Inn, Holiday Inn Express, Le Méridien, Millennium): 0.5 miles to 2 km from DXB. Three of the four list a shuttle, and the two 3★ hotels face Terminal 3.
  • Port Saeed and Deira City Centre (Royal Continental, Aloft, Flora Creek, Marriott Marquis): 1.3–1.9 miles out, next to the mall and metro. Better for a night out or a city day.
  • Dubai Festival City (Holiday Inn & Suites, Crowne Plaza): 2–3 km away, connected to Dubai Festival City Mall. Best for families.

Getting to and from the terminal

Premier Inn runs a free shuttle to Terminal 3 every 30 minutes (drop-off only at Terminal 1). Millennium lists a free shuttle to Terminals 1 and 3, Holiday Inn Express a free airport shuttle, and Aloft a scheduled shuttle to Terminal 3. Everyone else is a short taxi ride. By metro, Holiday Inn Express is a short walk from Emirates Metro Station, and Flora Creek is 5 minutes from Deira City Centre station.

Late arrivals and early departures

Check-in starts at 14:00 at Premier Inn, Holiday Inn Express, Millennium and Flora Creek, and 15:00 elsewhere. Most hotels here ask you to tell them your arrival time in advance, and Premier Inn's check-in closes at 23:30, so message the hotel if you land after 23:30. For departures, Premier Inn's check-out window opens at 01:00 and Holiday Inn Express runs to 14:00; everywhere else it is 12:00.

What costs extra

  • Taxes and fees: room prices exclude a 10% service charge, 5% VAT, a 7% municipality fee and a Dubai tourism fee of AED 10 (Premier Inn, Holiday Inn Express), AED 15 (Millennium, Aloft, Holiday Inn & Suites) or AED 20 (Le Méridien, Marriott Marquis, Crowne Plaza) per room per night.
  • Breakfast: included at Holiday Inn Express; €14 (Premier Inn), €17 (Aloft), €19 (Holiday Inn & Suites), €20 (Millennium), €28 (Crowne Plaza), €32 (Le Méridien), €36 (Marriott Marquis).
  • Deposits: AED 500 by card at Millennium, AED 300 in cash at Flora Creek.
  • Extra beds: free for under-13s at Crowne Plaza; AED 75–184 a night elsewhere; none at Premier Inn.

Making the most of a layover

With 8 hours or more, you can leave the hotel: compare Dubai city tours, book Burj Khalifa tickets for the observation deck, or take one of the Dubai dinner cruises on the creek. The rates above are for mid-January, the cooler winter season.

Premier Inn (to Terminal 3 every 30 minutes, drop-off only at Terminal 1), Millennium Airport Hotel (Terminals 1 and 3) and Holiday Inn Express. Aloft Dubai Creek lists a scheduled shuttle to Terminal 3. Marriott Marquis's free shuttle goes to DCC Mall and Kite Beach, not the airport.
