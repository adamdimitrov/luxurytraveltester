6 Best Helicopter Tours in Dubai in 2026 (Price per Minute Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
6 helicopter flights compared on flight time, route, price per minute and pickup
Last updated: Nov 16, 2026

For most visitors the right Dubai helicopter ride is the 12-minute Palm flight at €175: it covers the Palm, Atlantis, Burj Al Arab and Burj Khalifa, the four sights most people book a helicopter for. If you want more air time in a small group, the 17-minute Palm tour starts at €233, caps the group at 5 and adds The World Islands, Dubai Mall and Meydan.

Here is what surprised us when we lined up every flight: price per minute barely moves. The 12-, 22- and 30-minute flights all land between €14.57 and €14.74 a minute, so a 30-minute flight is not a bulk deal, it just costs more. What you are really choosing is the route. Old Dubai, the Creek and the Dubai Frame only appear on the 22- and 30-minute flights.

Below are the 6 options side by side: flight minutes, price, € per minute, landmarks covered, helipad and pickup. Filter by short flights, best value per minute, long routes or combos, and jump to the table if you just want the numbers.

This is the flight we would send most first-timers on. For €175 you get 12 minutes in the air from the HeliDubai helipad at the Dubai Police Academy, which sits right by Burj Al Arab. That position matters: you lift off by Burj Al Arab and fly straight over the Palm and Atlantis, so little of the 12 minutes is spent getting to the good part.

The route then runs inland to Burj Khalifa, the Dubai Canal and Business Bay. That is the full postcard set. What it does not reach is Old Dubai, the Creek and the Dubai Frame, which only appear on the 22- and 30-minute routes (#4 and #5).

The other reason it ranks first is flexibility. The same tour goes up to 30 minutes, offers private flights, and has a "with transfer" option that picks you up anywhere in Dubai. Compared with the Atlantis departure (#3), it costs €1.52 less for the same 12 minutes and gives you a pickup option.

Catches: check-in is 45 minutes before take-off, you must give each passenger's weight (safety limit 115 kg, nobody 140 kg or over flies), and the photos taken around the flight are an extra purchase.

Which Landmarks Each Flight Length Covers

Because the per-minute price is flat, the route is the only thing that should decide your flight length. Here is the rule of thumb from the six itineraries:

  • 12 minutes: the postcard set. Palm Jumeirah, Atlantis, Burj Al Arab and Burj Khalifa. From the Police Academy helipad the route also crosses the Dubai Canal and Business Bay.
  • 15–17 minutes: adds The World Islands, Port Rashid and Jumeirah Beach. The 17-minute HeliDubai route also passes Dubai Mall, Mall of the Emirates and the Meydan Racecourse.
  • 22 minutes: the first length that reaches Old Dubai: the heritage area with its wind towers, the Old Souk, Dubai Creek and the Dubai Frame.
  • 25–30 minutes: adds more of the west side: Dubai Marina, Jumeirah Lakes Towers, Emirates Hills and Media City (the 22-minute list already names Ain Dubai and Jumeirah Lakes).

If you are also going up the Burj Khalifa, the 12-minute flight overlaps with what you will see from the observation deck, so the 17- or 22-minute routes add more new views. Our Burj Khalifa ticket comparison covers the deck options.

Before You Fly: Check-In, ID, Weight Limits and Extras

  • Arrive 45 minutes early. #1 and #3 ask for check-in 45 minutes before take-off, #4 runs a 45-minute safety check, and the Atlantis flight can release your slot if you are late.
  • Bring ID. The Atlantis flight (#3) and the combo (#6) want an original passport or ID and refuse photocopies. The HeliDubai flights accept a passport or ID card, and #1 and #4 accept a copy.
  • Know your weight. Limits differ: 110 kg per passenger on shared Atlantis flights (or buy a second seat), 115 kg on #1 and #6, under 120 kg on #4, 140 kg on #5. #1 asks for every passenger's weight at booking.
  • Leave these behind: selfie sticks on every flight, tablets on #3 and #4, hats and high heels on #1, lenses of 400 mm or longer on #4.
  • What costs extra: return transfers on #2 and #5 (add-on at checkout), the "with transfer" option on #1, and flight photos and videos sold after landing.
  • Haze can move your slot. Winter mornings can be hazy, and weather can push a flight back. Book an early slot on a day with some slack, and use the free cancellation (24 hours on #1–#5, 2 days on #6) if the forecast looks hazy.
  • Age: children must be at least 2 on the HeliDubai flights (#1, #2, #4, #5) and 3 on the combo; #4 also needs children to weigh at least 16 kg.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is a helicopter ride in Dubai?

From €175 per person for 12 minutes (#1). The 17-minute Palm tour is €233, the 22-minute flight €321 and the 30-minute flight €437. That works out at about €14.60 a minute for the 12-, 22- and 30-minute flights, and €13.73 at 17 minutes.

Is a longer helicopter tour better value?

Not per minute. The 30-minute flight costs €14.57 a minute, the 12-minute flight €14.61. The lowest rate is the 17-minute tour at €13.73 (check you select the 17-minute option). Book a longer flight only for the route: 22 minutes adds Old Dubai and the Creek, 30 minutes adds Dubai Marina and Emirates Hills.

Is a 12-minute helicopter ride in Dubai worth it?

Yes, if you mainly want the Palm, Atlantis, Burj Al Arab and Burj Khalifa. Taking off next to Burj Al Arab means the Palm is under you almost straight away. It will not reach Old Dubai or the Marina.

Where do Dubai helicopter tours take off from?

Four of the flights here use the HeliDubai helipad at the Dubai Police Academy in Al Sufouh, near Burj Al Arab. The Atlantis flight takes off and lands at Atlantis The Palm, which suits guests at Palm Jumeirah hotels.

Do Dubai helicopter tours include hotel pickup?

Only some. #1 has a "with transfer" option covering anywhere in Dubai, #2 and #5 sell return transfers as an add-on at checkout, and the desert combo includes pickup. For the others, make your own way to the helipad.

Will I get a window seat?

Not guaranteed on a shared flight. The Atlantis helicopters have 6 seats with 3 at a window. For better odds, book a private flight or one of the tours capped at 5 guests (#2 and #5).

Can I combine a helicopter ride with a desert safari?

Yes. The helicopter and desert combo costs €272 with hotel pickup, dune bashing, sandboarding, a camel ride and a 12-minute flight from Palm Jumeirah. For evening camps with dinner, see our desert safari comparison.
