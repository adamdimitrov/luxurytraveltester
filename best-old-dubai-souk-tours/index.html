10 Best Old Dubai Souk Tours in 2026 (Souks, Abra Rides and Street Food Compared)

By Adam Dimitrov, Founder
10 Old Dubai tours compared on price, food tastings, abra ride, museums and pickup
Last updated: Nov 20, 2026

For most people the best Old Dubai tour is the €20 Old Town, Abra Cruise, Museums and Souks tour: 2.5–4 hours from Al Seef through Al Fahidi, a private abra across the Creek, museum entry, 4 tastings and a briefing on real souk prices before you haggle, with guides in 7 languages. Only have 2 hours? The 4.93-rated small-group tour (€25) fits 6 tastings into a loop that ends where it starts.

Almost every tour walks the same line: Al Fahidi's wind-tower houses, an abra across Dubai Creek, then the Spice and Gold Souks in Deira. What separates them is how much food is really in the price (from none to 8 street-food items), whether an Emirati house or museum is included, and where you start: a metro exit, a heritage café or your hotel lobby.

The common trap: several tours put shawarma and falafel in the name but serve them only on the premium option. The table below shows what each from-price really buys, from €11 to €29.

Practical Tips: Getting There, What to Bring and What Costs Extra

Getting there

The walking tours start on the Bur Dubai side of the Creek. Sharaf DG Metro (Green Line), Exit 4 is the meeting point for #7 and #8 and the nearest station for #9 at Al Seef. Several tours end in Deira near Al Ras Metro, so you can take the metro back rather than a taxi. Only #6 includes hotel pickup as standard; #1, #3, #4, #7 and #8 offer it as an option, #2 only on its private version.

What to bring

Comfortable shoes, a sun hat and sunglasses are on almost every tour's list. Baby strollers and large bags are not allowed on #3 and #7, and #4 and #5 do not take large bags either, so leave shopping bags at the hotel. Most of these walks are not suitable for wheelchair users or people with limited mobility; the vehicle-based #6 involves the least walking.

What costs extra

Tips are listed as extra on most tours. Watch the options: shawarma and falafel on #1 and #3, karak tea and samosa on #5, and the Dubai Frame ticket on #6 all depend on what you select at booking. Anything you buy in the souks is extra, which is why #1's price briefing earns its keep.

Making a day or a stay of it

Old Dubai pairs well with an evening dinner cruise, and the desert is a separate half day (see our desert safaris comparison). To wake up next to the souks, compare the best hotels on Dubai Creek or the heritage-style boutique hotels in Dubai.

What is the best Old Dubai tour?

For most visitors, the Old Town, Abra Cruise, Museums, Souks and Street Food Tour from €20: 2.5–4 hours through Al Fahidi and the Spice and Gold Souks, with a private abra, museum entry, 4 tastings and 7 guide languages. If you only have 2 hours, the 4.93-rated small-group tasting tour from the Arabian Tea House (€25) is the better fit.

Which Dubai street food tour includes the most food?

The Old Dubai Premium Guided Tour with Street Food (from €29) includes shawarma, falafel, karak tea, samosa, rogag, luqaimat, camel milk ice cream and lemon mint juice, plus coffee and dates, all in the base price. On several other tours the shawarma and falafel are only on the premium option.

Is the abra ride included on Old Dubai tours?

Yes, on all ten tours compared here the abra across Dubai Creek is included. Tour #1 uses a private abra with priority boarding; #8 uses a shared one.

How long is an Old Dubai souk tour?

Walking tours run 2 to 4 hours. The shortest is 2–2.5 hours (#2), most are about 3 hours, and the hotel-pickup tour with the Blue Mosque and Dubai Frame takes 5–6 hours including driving.

Do Old Dubai tours include hotel pickup?

Only the Creek, Souks, Blue Mosque and Frame tour includes hotel pickup as standard (not from Jebel Ali, Discovery Gardens, Investment Park, Expo City or Silicon Oasis). Several walking tours offer pickup as an option; the rest meet at Al Fahidi, Al Seef or Sharaf DG Metro, Exit 4.

Is there an evening Old Dubai tour?

Yes: the Evening Heritage Walk from €11 meets at Marsa Al Seef, crosses the Creek by abra and visits the Gold and Spice Souks over about 4 hours. It includes no food or museum tickets, so eat before or after.

Are Old Dubai walking tours suitable for kids and wheelchair users?

Most are not suitable for wheelchair users or people with limited mobility. Age limits vary: the Al Khayma tours (#3, #4) exclude under-2s and the €16 version (#5) excludes under-5s; strollers are not allowed on #3 and #7.

We compared 10 bookable Old Dubai tours that cover Al Fahidi, the abra across Dubai Creek and the Deira souks, from an €11 evening walk to a 6-hour tour with hotel pickup. Each was scored on price and price per hour, length, food tastings in the base price, abra type, Emirati house or museum entry, group format, start time and meeting point or pickup.
